# 006 — IO region with planes and plane vias (picture, unrouted)

Level shifters, RN arrays and connectors are unrouted here; the caps on B have their GND/+3V3 vias.
The VIO pins (pin 6 of each shifter) sit on the inner column, the +3V3 pins on the outer column, so an
In2 island for VIO would need notches for every +3V3 via. VIO is therefore planned as F/B tracks.
