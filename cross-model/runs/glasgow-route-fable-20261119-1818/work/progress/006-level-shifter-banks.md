# 006 – level-shifter banks (16 x SN74LVC1T45), scripted

Worked out the topology on paper first: with the pin order of the SC-70 parts and the resistor arrays, the A-side
nets (X) are planar on F.Cu, but the B-side nets (Y) of the two columns swap order pairwise and the DIR pins face the
wrong way. So:
- F.Cu: all 16 X nets (RN -> pin 3, the second column through the gap between rows) and the Y nets of the second
  column, no vias.
- B.Cu: Y nets of the first column (via beside pin 4, lane between the decap rows, via at the resistor pad) and all 16
  DIR lines (two lanes per row gap, via beside pin 5).
- Per part: +3V3, VIO and GND vias inside the footprint (step 003), decaps directly underneath.
Generated by script for both banks (172 items, `bt probe`: 6 errors at the two end rows where RN9/RN4 sit 0.7 mm from
the part, fixed by bringing those two DIR lines down the side on F.Cu; then all clear). Remaining ends joined with
`bt route`: 23 nets on F.Cu with no vias and 7 on B.Cu, all first pass.
