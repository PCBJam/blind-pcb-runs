# 000b — RP2040 area at placement (no copper)

Close-up of the RP2040, flash, USB-C, LDO and the level-shifter area, before any routing. This is the reference for the escape problems: the six key GPIOs (pins 31-37) come off the north edge, USB and QSPI off the west edge, and the flash sits directly under the chip's bottom-left corner.
