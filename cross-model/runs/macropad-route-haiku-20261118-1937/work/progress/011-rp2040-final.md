# 011 — RP2040 area in the final state

Picture 011-rp2040-final.png. Pours: GND on F.Cu and B.Cu with 61 stitching vias on a 4 mm grid. The exposed pad has four GND vias. The LDO output is 0.3 mm to the first decoupling cap (widened after the 3V3 route; see NOTES). The remaining 3V3 stubs are 0.2 mm.

Notes on what is left in this area:
- LED_EN has 12.6 mm at 0.127 mm (the fab minimum). It cannot get 0.2 mm now that the 3V3 copper is on both layers (tested).
- QSPI SD3 (24.7 mm) and SCLK (19.1 mm) are much longer than SD2 (10.2 mm).
- +1V1 pin 50 reaches the rest of the 1V1 net by an 8 mm loop round the USB resistors.
