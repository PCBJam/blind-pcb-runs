# 000c — key field at placement (no copper)

Six MX hot-swap sockets (pads on B.Cu only), six SK6812 LEDs and their decoupling caps on B.Cu. The GPIO pads are at the left-top of each socket, the GND pads at the right-top. The channel between the two rows is free of pads on both layers, but the key holes (NPTH) are in the way of most lanes.
