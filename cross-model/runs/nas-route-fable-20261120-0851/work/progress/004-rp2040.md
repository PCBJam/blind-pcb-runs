# 004 – RP2040 corner, resets, Ethernet/USB
- Ethernet: four 100 Ω pairs (0.127/0.2) on F.Cu from the CM4 through the flow-through ESD arrays to the RJ45 pins, lanes kept 1.7 mm off the CM4 mounting hole (its pad clearance). USB2 from the CM4 straight north to the common-mode choke, 0.15/0.15.
- J3's PCIe lanes re-laned a second time: straight south and the diagonal only at y≈100–110, because the first re-lane put three pairs under the U11 buck where no via (EN, GND) could land. The +1V2 feed is now a 2.2 mm F.Cu strip (no In2 "chimney" for a pair to cross).
- PERST# to the M.2 sockets: chain on F.Cu under the four connector bodies (enters each pin 50 from the body side), one trunk at y=84.3 south of the 12 V bus, B.Cu only for the feed from U12 and three short hops under the bus. Test-point and LED links under the connectors moved to short B.Cu hops so the chain is not crossed.
- RP2040: first try with `bt route` on all its nets failed (11 of 22, a 12 mm neck at 0.127, supply links ringed round the chip and walled the pins in) – thrown away. Second try by hand from the pin order: fans 1–3 leave north, turn east and peel into the headers in order with no crossing; QSPI as two nested groups round the flash; USB between the decoupling caps; status LEDs in a band south of the pull-ups; supply pins to their caps plus a via into a small +3.3VSTB island on In2.
