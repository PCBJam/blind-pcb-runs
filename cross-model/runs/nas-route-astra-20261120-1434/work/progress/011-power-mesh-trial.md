# Five-volt feed and a bus-strip trial

A room-temperature sheet-resistance model estimated about 66 mΩ between the 5 V inductor and the CM4 power pins. I added a wide In2 feed in the corridor south of the backside Ethernet links, with parallel vias at the supply and module. The estimate fell below 7 mΩ. I also tried segmented inner strips along the 12 V and fan buses, keeping ground underneath the backside differential pairs. Those extra strips improved the remote load estimates by only a few milliohms. This picture records that trial.
