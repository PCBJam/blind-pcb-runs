# Discarded fan-supply perimeter

I tried a front-and-back 5 V path around the south perimeter. Signal tracks and mounting-hole clearances interrupted the pours. Additional stitching gave little reduction in estimated fan-feed resistance. I discarded this perimeter layout. The working board instead uses a 2.4 mm In2 connection around the end of the high-speed routing, and two 1.6 mm parallel paths for the 12 V bus. The extracted resistance fell to about 24–26 mΩ for the remote fan headers and 35 mΩ for the furthest buck input.
