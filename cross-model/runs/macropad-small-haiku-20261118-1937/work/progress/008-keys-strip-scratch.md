# 008 key strip: first attempts (scratch)

Routed the six key nets straight from the switch pads and the RP2040's east pins, one call, `--order escapes-first`, on the board in /work and a scratch copy.

Result: 5 of 6 connected (KEY3 boxed in). The top keys (pins 35..37, south of the pin row) must cross the bottom keys (pins 31..34, north of the row), so the lanes at the 0.4 mm pin pitch block each other. Scratch copy only; not applied.
