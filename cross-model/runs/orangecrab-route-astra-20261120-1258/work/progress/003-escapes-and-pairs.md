# BGA escapes and protected routes

The memory clock, UDQS, and the connector-side USB pair have coupled inner-layer trunks and coordinated via sites. Ground and power stubs now land on the inner pours. Near the dense FPGA ground field, I thinned the candidate ground-via pattern to leave space for signals and power. KiCad counted 301 remaining connections and the same 354 fixed-placement/library findings as at the start. The first whole-board routing exchange returned an empty session despite reporting completion. I discarded it after checking the pin and copper counts.
