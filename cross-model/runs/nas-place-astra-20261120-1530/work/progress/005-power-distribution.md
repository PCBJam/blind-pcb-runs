The switch pin escapes and a second capacitor placement allowed all 15 long PCIe pairs to be routed from common centerlines. This view shows the In2 distribution copper. Each M.2 connector has a separate 3.3 V region, fed by its own converter. The protected 12 V supply has a wide trunk. Short, wider tracks serve the switch nodes and output capacitors, and multiple vias transfer current to the plane regions.
