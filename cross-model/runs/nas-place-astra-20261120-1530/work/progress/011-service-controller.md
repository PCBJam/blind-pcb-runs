I revised the service-controller block after finding that its USB data nets had taken 35 and 43 mm of track with different via counts. Rotating the RP2040 brought the USB pins to the connector side. Both main USB runs from the series resistors are now 6.7 mm on the front, and the duplicated receptacle contacts join at the socket. All six flash signals run on the front, and the crystal and load capacitors are on the opposite edge. The controller’s other pins have staggered via escapes.
