# 005 – DDR3 bus complete (except slow RESET#)

South-side data nets (upper byte, D0, UDM, both masks) are in:
- The autorouter could connect them but only with 13–25 mm detours, because the planned U4 slots made nets cross (D13/D15/LDM). I re-assigned slots at the U4 end so that the order of nets leaving the FPGA's south-east corner equals the order of the slots on each layer:
  D13 and D0 now use B.Cu, D13/D15 reach their bottom-row balls through U4's centre channel on B.Cu, LDM comes up the centre channel on F.Cu, D2 runs on F.Cu below the oscillator and drops to In2 right in front of U4.
- Under the FPGA's bottom edge six In2 nets leave in fixed lanes; the four front-layer exits (D8, D15, LDQS±) get a staggered pocket of vias between the CK+ and CKE exits. All coordinates generated by script against my own clearance checker, then `bt apply --checked`.

Result: all 49 DDR nets connected, 0–2 vias each. Lengths before tuning: address/control 7.6–16.9 mm, CK ≈17.7 mm to the ball, lower byte 8.3–15.0 mm, upper byte 11.3–17.2 mm (LDM 19.8 mm).
