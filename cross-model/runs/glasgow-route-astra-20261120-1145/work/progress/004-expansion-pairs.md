# J5 expansion routing

The thirteen FPGA expansion pairs now fan out as seven pairs on the front and six on the back. The back pairs change layers together near the BGA and the upper row of J5. Established the first two rows from a bt fanout trial, then assigned individual dogbones and paired bends to preserve ordering through the deeper rows. The first geometry probe caught conflicts between the deeper escape vias and the bypass capacitors. Staggering the front bends and changing several back-side dogbones cleared the geometry.
