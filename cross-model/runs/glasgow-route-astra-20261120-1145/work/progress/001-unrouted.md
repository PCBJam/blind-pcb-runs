# Unrouted Rev C3 board

The 80 × 49 mm outline holds 272 fixed footprints. The FX2 USB controller and oscillator are at the left, the 0.8 mm pitch FPGA is in the centre, and the two banks of translators, adjustable supplies and current monitors are to the right. Both inner layers are designated as power layers. The starting board has zero tracks, vias and zones. The image shows the original ratsnest.
