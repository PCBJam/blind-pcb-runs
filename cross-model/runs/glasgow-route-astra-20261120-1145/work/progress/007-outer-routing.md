# First general routing pass

On a working copy, a 0.10 mm search grid and 0.45/0.20 mm vias let the short-first bt pass complete 116 of 186 outstanding nets in 341 seconds. It also retained partial connections. Failures clustered at FPGA escapes, the FX2, and the translator network. Ground and supply planes were reserved during this pass. The image shows the populated signal layers before the remaining congestion was resolved.
