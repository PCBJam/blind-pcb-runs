# Congested digital channels

The second general-routing trial reused the short local tracks and protected USB, crystal and expansion routing. KiCad reported 98 open connections, eight dangling track warnings, and two dangling via warnings. The FPGA, its FX2 interface, and the two translator arrays account for most of the crowded escapes in this view. I retained the copper as a checkpoint for a finer search that can use the third layer at a higher routing cost.
