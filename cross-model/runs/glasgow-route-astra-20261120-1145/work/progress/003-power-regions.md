# Return and supply planes

In1 is a dedicated ground reference. In2 distributes 3.3 V, the FPGA core supply, protected 5 V, and the two independently adjustable bank supplies. Each bank supply has a branch between translator columns. The 5 V corridor feeds both output regulators around the lower and right edges. An In2 ground region backs the rear expansion traces. These initial fills precede the plane vias and signal routing.
