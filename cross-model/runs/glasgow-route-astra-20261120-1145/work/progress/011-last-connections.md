# Last FPGA connections

The routing checkpoint has three open connections. Local reroutes restored the nets they displaced, and a fresh fill checked whether the split supply copper still joined. Several short escapes now use In1 in the dense digital region. The USB corridor and the main expansion runs retain their ground reference. A point-by-point comparison of the schematic and PCB netlists also confirmed identical electrical grouping for all 1,063 reference and pad-number combinations, including nets with different names.
