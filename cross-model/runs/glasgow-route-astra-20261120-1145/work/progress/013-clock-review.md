# Reference clock reroute

The first complete solution sent part of CLKREF around the expansion header. Its total copper length, including the test-point branch, exceeded 110 mm. The first shortening pass brought it to 67 mm. Retaining the local receiver escapes and routing the clock within the digital section reduced it to about 30 mm. Rejoined the affected 3.3 V copper and confirmed zero opens after refill. This picture isolates CLKREF across all four layers.
