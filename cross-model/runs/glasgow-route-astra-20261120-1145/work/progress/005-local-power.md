# Current sensing and local power

Routed the two regulator feedback networks and mirrored the load-current paths on the back. Wide traces tie each set of populated and optional shunt footprints together. A separate narrow sense trace leaves the populated 0.15 Ω shunt pad for the monitor filter. Added local ground and supply vias for bypass capacitors, IC pins, and headers throughout the board. The image shows bank A, including its regulator, shunts and monitor input components.
