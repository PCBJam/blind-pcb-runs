# 003 – shifter cells, DA lines, +5V trunk by hand; first global autoroute pass

Pictures: `003-autoroute-pass1-top.png`, `003-autoroute-pass1-bottom.png` (green dashes = still open).

What I did
- Level-shifter grid: all 16 cells generated by script (X, DIR, Y of each shifter in the 1.1 mm
  channel between rows, three tracks per channel, direction lines up through one via column).
- A second autoroute attempt for the 16 direction lines (DA/DB) was thrown away: 12/16 after 309 s,
  DA0 with a 39 mm detour. Reason: the PLL1 filter parts on the back blocked the only way north.
  I moved R32, R39, R43, C19, C33, C92, drew DA3-DA7 on B.Cu and DA0-DA2 on In2 by hand.
- `bt plane` for GND/+3V3/+5V/VIOA/VIOB: a stub and via per SMD power pad into the planes.
- +5V: 0.8 mm B.Cu trunk from the bottom band up to both VIO regulators.
- Then `bt route --nets "*"` (F.Cu/B.Cu only, 1100 s budget): 83 of 119 remaining nets connected.

Result
- Still open after this pass: 36 nets (I2C, status LED lines, some Z/P/Y links, ENVA/ENVB, ALERT,
  SYNC, a few QA/QB/DB, +1V2, VIO_AUX and plane stubs). Next: targeted passes and hand work.
