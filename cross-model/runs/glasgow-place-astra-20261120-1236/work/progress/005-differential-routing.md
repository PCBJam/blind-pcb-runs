The USB data pair has a direct top-layer path to the FX2. I drew coupled routes for all 13 expansion pairs in header pin order. Seven reach the FPGA balls on top, with 0.15 mm escapes at the 0.8 mm grid. The other six use adjacent vias at the header and paired runs on the reverse, leaving their short FPGA escapes for completion. Copper probing caught conflicts between neighboring diagonal runs and the outer ball necks. Adjusting the lane heights and aligning each breakout with its ball gap cleared all 234 proposed pieces.
