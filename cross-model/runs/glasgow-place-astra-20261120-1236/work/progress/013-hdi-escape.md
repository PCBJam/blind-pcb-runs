Adjacent-layer laser vias gave access to signal-routing space under the FPGA and on the backside of the I/O banks without a through via blocking all four layers. I used 0.35/0.15 mm laser vias in the 0.135 mm outer dielectric. The broader 3.3 V plane and these local joins connected all separated 3.3 V groups. KiCad reported 21 remaining opens. This version requires an HDI fabricator and filled, capped in-pad vias.
