# Arduino Mega 2560 layout review

**Select C for the first fabrication run.** It connects the full netlist, gives the 5 V regulator useful copper on both sides, and keeps the USB turnover short. Before releasing it, resolve the shared connector and crystal assembly clearances and inspect the clipped legend. For a product expected to power shields from the barrel jack, qualify the regulator at the specified maximum input voltage, load and ambient temperature. Those limits were absent from the job.

Overall scores reflect suitability for ordering. In particular, an open power rail outweighs good routing on an individual signal.

## What was checked

I opened all four projects in KiCad 10.0.8, compared footprints and pad nets, inspected the copper layers, and ran project DRC on scratch copies both as supplied and after a zone refill. There are no project DRC exclusions. The four files agree on all 66 footprint positions, rotations, sides and pad assignments. Placement is therefore **null on every board**. The supplied set contains neither an independent unrouted master nor designer NOTES.md files. Coordinates below are KiCad absolute **(x, y) in mm**, with y increasing down the page.

Missing connections are reported separately from the DRC error column. The area and reference columns use the **saved** fills. I intersected routed centerlines with opposite-side GND polygons, tracks, pads and via lands, excluding the supply rails and GND from the signal-length denominator. This measures where a return can run directly under a trace. Same-side ground and AC return through a bypassed supply can help at gaps. The measure gives neither loop inductance nor an EMC test result. Counts of GND vias include local pad returns as well as stitching. Separate filled polygons can connect through vias on the other layer.

### USB measurements

J1 is a reversible USB-C receptacle feeding the full-speed ATmega16U2, U4. Each data line has two connector contacts, a protection branch to RV1 or RV2, and a 22 Ω series element in RN2 at (109.79, 94.58). The receiver pins are U4.29 and U4.30 at (115.23, 97.11) and (115.23, 97.61). I followed each contact through its resistor to the corresponding pin. Summing all copper on a branched net would count the unused connector leg and protection stub in the transmission path.

Lengths are approximate in-plane pad-center paths, excluding the resistor body and via barrels. Two through transitions on D− add roughly 3.2 mm of vertical travel on a 1.6 mm board. The 0.60 mm proximity measure compares how long the pair travels together. It does not establish 90 Ω differential impedance. With this short full-speed link, the return at a layer change, the protection branches and the changing pair separation matter more than a fraction of a millimetre of planar skew. U3 and U4 have internal program flash. The relevant off-chip clocks are the oscillators and the SPI/programming headers. U3 drives resonator Y1 on XTAL1/XTAL2. U4 drives Y2 on Net-(U4-XTAL1) and Net-(U4-PC0), with C16/C17 as the 22 pF load capacitors.

### Findings shared by all four

- **Mechanical and assembly.** The common 12 courtyard errors include Y2 against C16, C17, C11, C13 and R2, plus J2 against J3, J6 and D3. The body outlines help interpret them. C16's nominal body ends at y = 102.724 and Y2's flange starts at y = 103.301, a 0.58 mm gap. The J2 and J3 F.Fab drawing extents leave only about 0.22 mm between them near x = 118.55. Check the fitted header housings and programmer plug access here. FID4 is under U3 at (148.53, 103.31) and will be covered after assembly.
- **Silkscreen.** Each board has 37 silk-overlap, eight silk-over-mask and five silk-at-edge warnings. Examples include J5 artwork at (191.70, 79.88), U3's reference over FID4, and bottom legend over J2.6 at (113.47, 86.70). The other 51 common warnings are embedded-footprint versus installed-library differences. These account for all 101 common warnings.
- **Fabrication basis.** I assumed standard 1 oz outer copper, 1.6 mm FR-4 and a routed profile. JLCPCB lists 0.10/0.10 mm track/space and 0.20 mm routed-edge clearance for the relevant process. Via, plated component and non-plated holes have separate requirements. All four use through vias with drills of at least 0.30 mm. J1's four shell slots are 0.60 mm wide with a nominal 0.20 mm minimum annulus. Independently measuring different-net track copper to drilled edges gave minima across the four boards of 0.277 mm to a via hole, 0.382 mm to a plated component hole and 0.213 mm to a non-plated hole. A's saved *zone* clearance failure at J1 is separate. I successfully exported both copper layers, both masks, both silkscreens, Edge.Cuts and separate PTH/NPTH drill files for C. [JLCPCB fabrication capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)
- **Circuit and load assumptions.** U1 is an NCP1117-5.0 linear regulator and U6 is an LP2985-3.3. Q1 selects the USB supply. The design has no switching-regulator hot loop. The important loops are the input and output capacitor returns, MCU switching current, the clocks, and USB/shield transients. A 0.5 A shield load is a useful scale given F1's MF-MSMF050 part number. At 9 V on U1's input pin and 0.5 A through U1, the regulator would dissipate about **2 W**. A large tab connection helps, but the actual load rating requires thermal validation. Fixed placement also limits MCU bypassing. U3.61 at (156.19, 104.31) is 12.03 mm in a straight line from the nearest 100 nF +5V pad, C7.1 at (155.98, 92.29).

## C — first, 8/10

- **EMI — 6/10.** The 98 GND vias include a return 0.75 mm from Y1.2 at (135.55, 116.53) and one 0.80 mm from C16.2 at (112.19, 103.12). The rear GND fill breaks into 23 polygons. At (145.20, 113.87), the rear ADC fan-out interrupts the reference under the front oscillator route. Of the 14.38 mm of XTAL1, 10.03 mm has no GND directly below it. At the top edge, front SCL runs along y = 79.00 over the 0.55 mm rear +5V feeder at y = 79.14 for much of its length. There is a 63.86 mm continuous interval without underlying GND. The +5V bend at (184.82, 79.14) clears the profile by only 0.501 mm. The shield return could also be shorter. RV1.1 at (108.97, 97.37) takes about 8.18 mm of 0.20 mm USHIELD copper to a J1 shell pad, changing layers at (105.26, 100.72).
- **Signal integrity — 7/10.** C keeps the largest fraction of RD+ beside RD− and keeps both 0.22 mm traces on the front over GND from RN2 to U4. Planar end-to-end skew is about 0.39 and 0.59 mm for the two connector orientations. Only the B7 branch uses the 1.88 mm rear crossover, between (106.26, 92.51) and (106.34, 94.36). The RN2-to-protection branches still extend about 2.1–2.2 mm. U4 to Y2 is approximately 8.01/8.15 mm with no vias. U3 to Y1 is 14.38/13.17 mm, also via-free, but over the ADC corridor described above. SCK2 from U4.15 to J2.3 is 19.26 mm with two transitions, including one close to the driver at (121.37, 95.61).
- **Power — 8/10.** J4.1 (109.26, 123.28) to D4.2 (117.17, 129.13) has a 1.05 mm main trace. D4.1 to U1.3 is 23.21 mm of 0.85 mm copper. U1.3 reaches C4.1 in 3.42 mm and bulk C1.1 in 11.89 mm. The +5V output tab at (103.21, 113.88) has about 70 mm² of front and 59 mm² of rear +5V pour, linked by four 0.75/0.35 mm vias outside the solder land. The 47 µF output capacitor C2.1 at (123.25, 120.02) is still a 16.13 mm path with two transitions. Locally, U3.10 to C8.1 is 2.55 mm at 0.35 mm width with a 4.31 mm explicit GND leg from C8.2 to U3.11. U6.5 to C15.2 is 2.44 mm at 0.45 mm width. The main tradeoff is remote distribution. There are 281 mm of 0.35 mm +5V trace across the network, supplemented by the 0.55 mm rear perimeter feeder. Heavy far-header loading deserves a voltage-drop check.
- **Routing and manufacturability — 8/10.** Both DRC runs find only the common 12 errors and 101 warnings. The narrowest routed trace is 0.20 mm and the default clearance is 0.15 mm. Of 200 vias, 183 are 0.60/0.30 mm. No via center lies in an SMD copper land. The sharp joins at D4.2 (117.17, 129.13) and C18.2 (141.36, 95.57) are inside their pads. The shared connector clearances and silk clipping remain the main assembly review items.

## B — second, 7/10

- **EMI — 7/10.** B has the strongest overall rear reference, a single 3,547 mm² GND fill, 134 GND vias, and 66% of front signal length directly over GND. C12, C16 and C17 have GND vias about 0.8 mm from their ground pad centers. C12's is at (112.27, 93.65). The entire 18.35 mm of front USBVCC trace has ground beneath it. Local slots still matter. SCL parallels the board edge for 66.46 mm at y = 79.00, with 0.566 mm copper-to-edge clearance. For 49.49 mm it loses underlying GND above the rear LED-anode route, Net-(D6-A), near (150.30, 79.00). PB1 has a 21.31 mm continuous reference gap across the ADC routes near (164.68, 119.88).
- **Signal integrity — 6/10.** The via-free front RD pair has continuous rear GND and small end-to-end planar skew. The 5.05 mm D− rear branch between (103.95, 92.60) and (108.13, 94.70) lacks front GND directly above it. The nearest GND layer connection at its first via is 4.54 mm away. With the A contacts active, that rear leg feeds the unused B7 contact. The RN2-to-RV branches are about 2.25/2.41 mm. On the clock side, U4 to Y2 is 8.16/8.15 mm, front only, with roughly 1 mm on each net lacking underlying GND. SCK2 also stays on the front for its 19.96 mm route. The main SPI clock PB1 reaches J12.3 in 21.90 mm, but also drives a header at J5.33 over a 64.31 mm path. An unused header branch is a substantial stub relative to the programming-header path.
- **Power — 6/10.** The 1.20 mm jack-to-D4 trace, 0.80 mm fused F1.1-to-Q1.3 connection and broad +5V distribution suit shield current. One U1-to-J5.1 path has about 87 mm at 0.80 mm width. C8.1 reaches U3.10 in 2.55 mm over GND. The weakness is the regulator. VCC from D4.1 (121.47, 129.13) to U1.3 (109.51, 111.58) travels 45.52 mm, including the detour through (101.20, 119.00). C1.1 to U1.3 alone is 32.63 mm. The local C4 path is a much better 3.47 mm. The tab has three 0.40 mm drills, but the rear receives the heat in traces. In a 10 × 10 mm window centered on the tab, B has only about 12 mm² of +5V copper on the front and 9 mm² on the rear. C has 65/59 mm² in the same window. B's 19.24 mm front path to output bulk C2 is another source of loop inductance.
- **Routing and manufacturability — 7/10.** B has the same DRC result as C, a measured minimum trace of 0.20 mm and a 0.20 mm default clearance. Its local rule enforces 0.25 mm routed-copper-to-hole clearance while accommodating the fixed J1 peg geometry. Most of the 221 vias are ordinary 0.60/0.30 mm through vias. Three 0.80/0.40 mm vias lie **in** the U1 tab land at x = 103.20, y = 112.70, 113.90 and 115.10. Four more 0.30 mm drills lie in U4's exposed pad, including (116.90, 96.10). These are potential solder drains on an ordinary reflow build. Resolve the paste and via treatment with the assembler. The 45° included join on XVCC at (103.00, 101.30) lands on a via, a lower-order issue than the seven pad drills.

## A — third, 5/10

- **EMI — 5/10.** Ground occupies both layers and 53 vias join or access it. The D− crossover vias at (106.43, 93.91) and (107.98, 95.32) have GND transitions within 1.69 and 1.32 mm, and RV1.1 reaches J1's shell on a broad 0.80 mm USHIELD trace in 4.54 mm. Elsewhere, back PB/ADC bundles and wide supplies interrupt the return. Front PB1 runs **27.70 mm continuously** without GND below it near (160.39, 120.36). Rear RESET hugs the left edge at x = 98.39 from y = 89.09 to 118.91, just 0.536 mm copper-to-edge. Net-(D6-A) from RN3 to D6 switches layers four times and has only 24.30 of 71.75 mm over opposite-side GND. Refilling shrinks the saved GND areas to 2,083/2,819 mm² front/rear.
- **Signal integrity — 6/10.** Both RN2-to-U4 traces are via-free over GND. Their planar mismatch is 1.59 mm. D+ stays on the front, while both J1 contact orientations send D− through the same two vias and 2.14 mm rear leg. The unused duplicate contacts and roughly 2.1–2.2 mm protection branches add stubs. Both oscillator circuits stay on the front. U4 to Y2 measures 8.16/8.15 mm and U3 to Y1 measures 15.72/13.87 mm. XTAL1 loses direct reference for 4.45 mm starting at the U3 end (146.53, 110.98). The most avoidable detour is PB1, U3.20 (140.86, 106.81) to J12.3 (161.35, 103.72). Those pads are only 20.72 mm apart, yet the electrical route is 70.87 mm with three vias, reaching (171.64, 120.74) before returning to the header.
- **Power — 8/10.** A gives the regulator the best heat spreading. In the same 10 × 10 mm tab window there is about 90/89 mm² of +5V copper, still 89/88 mm² after refill. Ten vias surround the tab region, including (100.27, 109.44) and (105.98, 117.69), outside the solder land. The jack-to-D4 path uses 1.00 mm copper. D4.1 to U1.3 is 25.46 mm at 0.80–1.00 mm. About 169 mm of the distributed +5V network is 1.27 mm wide, with 0.20–0.40 mm drops at light loads. F1.1 to Q1.3 is a direct 4.10 mm at 0.60 mm. Decoupling is less consistent. The 2.55 mm feed from C8.1 (138.55, 101.24) to U3.10 has no directly underlying GND, and C12.2's nearest ground transition is 4.79 mm away. After refill U3.99's GND thermal has just one spoke.
- **Routing and manufacturability — 4/10.** As supplied, all 499 clearance markers involve zones. J1's peg at (104.14, 90.67) has only **0.0255 mm** to the XVCC fill, and the mask openings at all four fiducials expose unrelated pour copper. Refill clears those findings and preserves connectivity. Four copper errors survive in addition to the common courtyard errors. RESET passes 0.4098 mm from the mounting cutout centered at (163.77, 124.04), inside the project's 0.50 mm allowance. U3.99 and coincident J1.A1/B12 at (105.22, 90.36) each report one of two required thermal spokes. There is also a 0.0003 mm dangling SCL segment at (129.22, 92.29). A has the fewest vias and uses generous drills, with seven 0.15 mm segments around the SPI header. There is a genuine 45° included bend on PE0 at (141.75, 97.66). The stored zone copper is the reason to hold the order.

## D — fourth, 1/10

- **EMI — 1/10.** D has a rear GND zone, no front GND zone and only eight GND vias out of 169. Less than 1% of routed back-side signal length is directly above front GND. The 61.86 mm rear SCL interval near (147.00, 79.76) illustrates the problem. More fundamentally, even the nominal GND network is open. U4's exposed pad (117.67, 96.86) is disconnected from U4.28 (115.23, 96.61), and U1.1 (109.51, 116.18) is disconnected from input bypass ground C4.2 (112.08, 114.78). No local supply loop can be relied on there. The two D− transitions near (106.77, 93.31) are over 9 mm from the nearest nominal GND via or plated GND pad. The VCC header feed runs at y = 130.62 with 0.553 mm outer-edge clearance.
- **Signal integrity — 3/10.** D matches RD+ and RD− to 6.81 mm each on the front. Short jogs in RD− near (111.84, 96.83) and (113.60, 98.25) achieve that match while only 1.16 mm of RD+ lies within 0.60 mm of RD−. The B7 contact changes layers twice around a 1.10 mm rear segment. The protection spurs remain about 2.1 mm. The 14.34 mm SCK2 route is via-free, and the U3-to-Y1 routes are 14.41/13.20 mm. Those individual successes cannot redeem the disconnected Y1.2 ground at (136.08, 117.06) or missing SPI data connections. For example, PB2 is open between U3.21 (140.86, 107.31) and J12.4 (163.89, 103.72).
- **Power — 1/10.** D routes the jack and diode in 1.00 mm copper, gives U1 a front +5V tab pour, and takes VCC to the local input capacitor in 3.25 mm. But it has **zero +5V tracks and zero +5V vias**. Its local +5V pour cannot supply the board. There are 29 missing +5V links and 35 missing GND links. Both sides of the U3.10/C8/U3.11 decoupling loop are open, at C8.1 (138.55, 101.24) and C8.2 (137.00, 101.24). Q1.2 (108.07, 104.80) is disconnected from the regulator output region. The LP2985 output at (126.62, 107.63) reaches C15 on a 0.15 mm trace, and the whole 39.71 mm of +3.3V track uses that width.
- **Routing and manufacturability — 1/10.** All 169 vias are ordinary 0.60/0.30 mm. The minimum routed width is 0.127 mm, including a 1.76 mm VCC neck into the RN5 sense divider at (115.44, 114.75). That low-current tap is a reasonable place to neck down. A doubling-back RESET join at (116.84, 116.42) falls inside RN5.1. The decisive result is **88 missing links after refill**, including 24 beyond the two main power nets. ADC0 is open from U3.97 (144.03, 95.65) to J10.8 (148.53, 129.12). Plating and etching the submitted artwork successfully would still produce a nonfunctional board.

## Ranking and authorship

**C > B > A > D.** C has the best combination of electrical closure, regulator copper, local bypass returns, USB routing and ordinary assembly. B earns its lead on ground continuity but loses to C on regulator heating and via-in-pad assembly risk. A's supply layout is worth preserving in a revision. Its stale fills and remaining copper DRC findings make it a worse order candidate than either C or B. D needs electrical completion.

**My human-layout guess is A, with 85% confidence.** The combination of deliberately shaped regulator and USB-input pours, an offset row pattern of large thermal vias, a broad shield-discharge path, and selective width changes from 1.27 mm distribution to fine SPI escapes looks like a board hand-routed and revised over time. The long PB1 detour and residual sliver of SCL look consistent with local edits. Geometry cannot establish authorship, and this inference was made after the engineering ranking.
