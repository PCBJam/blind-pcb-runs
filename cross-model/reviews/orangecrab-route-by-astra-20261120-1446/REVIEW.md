# OrangeCrab six-layer layout review

**Hold the fab order. A is the board I would take through final DFM.** It has complete connectivity, the best DDR3 timing geometry, two continuous ground references and an entirely through-drilled escape. Its saved fills and KiCad 10 refill behavior need repair. C is the other electrically complete candidate. It has good local regulator routing and the cleanest reproducible electrical DRC, but its drill files require a stacked HDI build. D and B have opens on essential interfaces.

The overall score is a release judgment. An open configuration clock can prevent the FPGA from booting regardless of the quality of the other routes. A custom lamination process also carries substantial weight for the specified standard six-layer order.

## What was checked

I loaded each PCB in KiCad 10.0.8, ran DRC with its accompanying project rules, checked saved and refilled copper on scratch copies, and exported Excellon drills. I compared footprint references, values, positions, orientations, sides and pad net assignments across all four. All 163 footprint placements agree, hence **placement is null for every board**. Each outline measures 50.80 × 22.86 mm. Coordinates below are absolute KiCad X, Y in mm. The supplied directories contain the boards and project settings. Schematics, the unrouted baseline and designers’ NOTES.md files were absent, so schematic parity and comparison to the original placement could not be verified.

Board-level DRC counts below use KiCad's normal per-track reporting. Missing connections are a separate report section. Excluded warnings are shown separately.

All four have 68 courtyard overlaps, one malformed J5 courtyard at (145.21, 98.88), 111 library-availability warnings, 51 library footprint mismatches and 39 undersize silk texts. One overlap is U4 (174.85, 102.40) with C74 (169.85, 102.05). A courtyard overlap calls for checking the actual bodies and assembly clearances. The silk example “RST” at (141.11, 111.64) is 0.60 mm tall against the project minimum of 0.80 mm and JLCPCB's published 1.0 mm legend height. These common findings carry the same weight across layouts.

A, B and D also report 84 edge-pad errors from the intentional J1/J2 castellated lands. For example, J1.1 has a 1.0 mm plated drill centered at (151.02, 91.39), cut by the edge at Y = 91.52. The order needs the castellated-hole option and review of the cut position. C has a scoped edge-clearance rule on J1/J2 pads. A's three additional copper-edge errors concern poured copper.

**Measurement conventions.** Lengths are planar track centerlines. For branched nets I followed the copper between the named pads, including in-pad travel where appropriate. Whole-net figures are identified separately. The estimates omit vertical barrel length, package delay and connector internals. Ground coverage is the intersection of track centerlines with the *saved filled polygons* on the stated adjacent layer. It detects splits and antipads but does not model fringing, lateral returns or local ground tracks. The proximity comparisons sample the positive leg at intervals of at most 0.08 mm and look for the negative leg on the same layer. They measure geometry, not impedance. There is no supplied DDR operating point, rail load budget or measured impedance coupon.

**Fabrication screen.** JLCPCB publishes 0.09/0.09 mm multilayer trace/space for 1 oz, with a local BGA fan-out allowance, and 0.15/0.25 mm minimum ordinary via drill/land at this board thickness. A 0.15 mm drill incurs additional cost. The 0.23 mm U3 ball lands also put finish selection in JLCPCB's ENIG range. Blind and buried vias require its advanced HDI process. I separately measured ordinary through-via drill edges to other-net inner-layer track edges against its published 0.20 mm inner hole-to-copper figure. The counts below are affected vias, counted once each, and exclude pours, pads and blind/buried holes. All four warrant DFM even where their own clearance rules pass. ## A — strongest electrical layout and best standard-process starting point

**EMI · 9/10.** In1 and In4 each have a single 891 mm² GND fill covering 77.1% of the outline. All tracks on those two layers are themselves GND. F and In2 therefore share an uninterrupted reference over most of the board, and B has its own adjacent ground. The In2 USB positive trunk projects onto In1 GND for 21.35 of 21.88 mm. The B-side `RAM_UDQS+` trunk has 13.96 of 14.48 mm over In4 GND. Of 441 vias, 141 carry GND and nine are within 2 mm of the perimeter. The connector-side USB positive transition at (139.39, 102.67) is 3.05 mm from the nearest GND via, although F and In2 can both return on In1. The positive USB track stays at least 2.90 mm inside the outline. The 48 MHz OSC1 output, from (167.45, 101.75) to U3.A9 (165.45, 100.65), has ground directly under its entire 3.11 mm run.

**SI · 9/10.** The connector nets `/IO/_USB_D_P` and `/IO/_USB_D_N` run together on In2 for 21.88 mm per leg. At (152.37, 94.49) they are 0.130 mm wide with 0.150 mm edge gap. Reversible J3 contacts, TP26/TP27 and the series resistors make total net length a poor skew metric. J3.A6→R21 and J3.A7→R29 measure 25.09 and 27.50 mm. The B6/B7 paths measure 26.34 and 25.09 mm. The test-pad branches add about 2.4 mm apiece. R21/R29→U3 adds 4.08/4.73 mm on F. The D+ pull-up at R20 is consistent with full-speed USB. The branches and absence of a specified stackup still preclude a 90 Ω sign-off.

DDR3 is A's clearest advantage. `RAM_D0…7` measure 15.32–15.85 mm, `RAM_D8…15` 15.84–15.91 mm, and A0…A15 14.99–15.56 mm of track. Each data bit uses two vias. `RAM_LDQS+/-` measure 15.850/15.850 mm and `RAM_UDQS+/-` 15.395/15.350 mm. The two strobe trunks have 0.15 mm edge gap on In2 and B respectively. The clock totals look matched at 21.704/21.707 mm, but include the R5/R13 termination branches. The actual U3→U4 paths end at J7 (173.25, 102.00) and K7 (173.25, 101.20) and measure 16.12/16.93 mm. That 0.81 mm receiver skew and the through-via stubs on inner transitions are worth carrying into timing analysis. Flash `SPI_CONFIG_SCK` is 15.88 mm from U3.U16 to U6.6 (166.75, 109.29) on a net with two vias and an R31 pull-up branch.

**Power · 9/10.** The TLV62569 bucks U1/U2/U7 feed L1/L2/L3 and the 1.1 V, 1.35 V and 3.3 V rails. Their top-layer switch connections are only 1.30/1.44/1.24 mm long. The nominal 0.121 mm tracks are embedded in local switch-node polygons of 1.89/1.93/1.99 mm². Each input capacitor shares continuous local F copper with **both** its regulator's VIN and GND pads, on their respective nets. U1.3 (151.14, 108.36) to C6.2 (151.03, 107.23) has 1.17 mm of track and the GND leg is 1.27 mm. The four pad centers enclose just 0.83 mm², a geometric measure of the available hot loop. C77 is 1.13 mm by track from charger U5.13 and its ground pad is 0.59 mm from a GND via. In3 supplies broad rail copper, including a 47.3 mm² VSYS region, a 464.3 mm² 3.3 V region and dedicated DDR VDD/VDDQ regions. Bottom FPGA decoupler C54 has 1.1 V and GND vias within 0.77/0.57 mm of its pad centers. The combination of short terminal loops and parallel copper matters more here than the narrow seed tracks. Load-dependent droop remains unverified.

**Routing and manufacture · 6/10.** All 441 vias are through holes with 0.15 mm drills and 0.28 or 0.30 mm lands. None has its drill center on a U3 or U4 ball land. Most track pieces are 0.121 mm. Some BGA escapes neck to 0.089 mm. An acute 46° P1.1V join on In3 at (161.20, 100.90) and a dangling 0.225 mm `RAM_A6` segment at (165.30, 97.90) merit cleanup. The independent fab screen found 120 through vias under 0.20 mm to an inner track, down to 0.154 mm from the GND drill at (164.87, 98.78) to `ECP5_VREF`. Thirteen via-to-via hole gaps violate the project's 0.25 mm rule, down to 0.202 mm.

The saved DRC includes 56 clearance, 13 hole-clearance and six mask-bridge errors, plus overlapping B.Cu GND zones with identical board-sized outlines and priority. A saved GND pour comes within 0.150 mm of the edge. An untouched refill was still stalled after five minutes. On a separate copy, replacing the **21 identical null zone identifiers** with unique identifiers, with the source geometry and rules held fixed, let KiCad refill and retain zero missing connections. The hole-clearance and mask-bridge errors disappeared. One clearance error survived, 0.0882 mm from B.Cu GND to U9.19 at (159.06, 106.92) against 0.0889 mm required. The duplicate zone, 84 castellated-pad errors and five dangling-via and three dangling-track warnings also survived. A needs a reproducible final refill, drill-clearance agreement and a check of the regenerated plots before release.

## C — connected, with a materially different fabrication process

**EMI · 7/10.** In1 and In4 carry 71.8% and 72.7% GND fill. There is also ground on F, In2 and B. This gives both outer sides a nearby reference, but 71.1 mm of tracks on In1 and 73.7 mm on In4 cut channels into those planes. At (165.13, 105.55) `RAM_CK+` has a 4.02 mm continuous run on In2 outside the In1 ground fill. C specifies 0.09 mm from In2 to In1 but a 1.078 mm core to In3, so losing the close plane here matters. In contrast, 19.10 of 20.19 mm of the In2 USB positive trunk project onto In1 ground. Its connector-side via at (139.76, 102.70) has a GND through via 0.72 mm away. There are 136 GND via objects, one within 2 mm of the perimeter. The positive USB track stays 2.93 mm inside the edge. OSC1→U3 is 2.46 mm entirely over In1 GND.

**SI · 7/10.** Both connector-side USB legs have 20.19 mm on In2, with 0.150 mm width and 0.130 mm gap at (148.91, 98.88). Two through vias per net serve the escapes and backside test access. J3.A6/A7→R21/R29 measure 21.39/22.69 mm and B6/B7 measure 23.73/21.39 mm. TP26/TP27 contribute branches of about 2.65/2.77 mm. On the FPGA side, D+ takes 2.72 mm on F from the resistor to the ball, while D− takes 3.38 mm with two vias. C specifies ENIG and dielectric geometry, a better starting point for impedance verification than an unspecified stack.

The actual U3→U4 clock legs measure **15.302/15.299 mm** despite their different termination branch lengths. Both strobe pairs are closely matched within themselves, 12.717/12.718 mm and 12.150/12.159 mm, with 0.153 mm trunk gaps. The rest of the timing group is weaker. D0…7 span 10.64–15.98 mm, D8…15 span 13.39–18.66 mm, and A0…A15 span 11.95–18.39 mm. DQ bits also use different layers and via depths. Each `RAM_LDQS+` end traverses three stacked via segments, for example at (162.33, 105.27). These spreads call for a package-plus-PCB timing budget at the chosen DDR rate. Flash SCK is 13.32 mm between U3 and U6, with four via objects on the net. Only 3.92 of its 6.95 mm on In2 lie above In1 GND.

**Power · 8/10.** U1/U2/U7 reach their inductors in 1.30/1.44/1.65 mm of 0.36 mm F copper. All three input capacitors share local VIN and GND fills with their switches. U1.2 and C6.1 both touch the same 14.81 mm² GND region, with a through via at (151.64, 107.85). C77.1 and U5.13 share a 1.61 mm² VBUS fill. J3.A4→U5.13 measures 7.70 mm by track. The VBUS and VBATT routes widen to 0.50 mm in places. In3 also carries 548 mm of track, dividing the power fills. Its 414 mm² of 3.3 V includes separate polygons of 151, 150 and 75 mm², and the largest P1.35V polygon is only 8.3 mm². Vias and routed links carry current between regions. P1.1V has 9.09 mm of 0.40 mm track but also 6.97 mm of 0.127 mm track across its branches. The local bypass geometry is strong. Distribution impedance deserves a load check.

**Routing and manufacture · 4/10.** There are no opens, active copper-clearance errors or dangling track/via warnings, including after refill. The 69 active errors are the shared courtyard findings. The J1/J2 edge rule is explicit. Two acute 45° joins away from pads and vias occur on `RAM_A10` at (170.48, 99.04) and `RAM_A6` at (168.44, 93.92). Most traces are 0.127 mm with six 0.089 mm segments. The through-hole clearance screen flags 151 vias, including 0.155 mm between a `RAM_A12` drill at (165.18, 99.38) and an In2 `RAM_A15` trace.

Of **858 via objects, 359 are through, 385 are 0.10 mm laser microvias and 114 are buried**. Excellon export produces six internal-span drill files in addition to PTH and NPTH, including In1–In4 and In2–In3. The source specifies filling and capping. There are via centers on 23 U3 and seven U4 ball lands. The 31 excluded co-located-hole warnings document intentional stacks, for example on DDR VDDQ at (176.96, 103.09). A waiver records intent. Building this board requires a fabricator to accept the exact sequential lamination, filled via stacks and BGA pad finish. It cannot be ordered as an ordinary through-drilled six-layer board.

## D — better power and planes than its open count suggests

**EMI · 5/10.** In1 is a track-free 870 mm² GND polygon. It references 24.64 of the 25.25 mm In2 USB positive route and all 2.46 mm of `REF_CLK`. In3 also has ground but is cut by 68.6 mm of routing. The bottom side faces split In4 power. Of the 13.23 mm B.Cu `RAM_LDQS+` run, 3.90 mm projects onto DDR VDD, 2.51 onto DDR VDDQ, 3.38 onto P1.35V and 2.40 onto P3.3V. Those changes of reference near (167.38, 107.44) make the return depend on rail-to-ground bypassing. There are 111 GND through vias, two within 2 mm of the perimeter. DRC still finds a disconnected GND fill component. The USB positive route comes within 2.00 mm of the edge at (136.56, 96.40).

**SI · 3/10.** The positive USB leg reaches R21, taking 34.04 mm from J3.A6 across a net with four vias and copper on F, In2, In3 and B. The negative connector net has just 3.36 mm of In2 track. R29.2 at (157.16, 94.73) and TP27 at (137.51, 100.98) are both disconnected from the connector-side copper. A short, relatively tidy R21/R29→U3 pair cannot rescue that link. The DDR clock reaches U4 with paths of 18.02/17.79 mm. The upper strobe legs measure 12.86/12.27 mm, but only 0.72 mm of the positive leg is within 0.35 mm of the negative leg on the same layer. D0…7 span 8.28–13.45 mm and D8…15 span 11.33–17.21 mm. `RAM_RESET#` is open from U3.L18 (160.45, 105.15) to U4.T2 (177.25, 96.40). Flash SCK does reach U6.6 in 15.87 mm. SD clock, command and DAT3 have opens.

**Power · 5/10.** U1→L1 and U2→L2 use short 0.40 mm switch traces. U7→L3 uses 1.77 mm of 0.20 mm track. U1.3→C6.2 is a 1.17 mm local VIN connection and their ground pads share a top pour, with a GND via at (151.64, 107.78). C54's supply via is just 0.08 mm from its backside pad center. The In4 rail regions distribute power to the FPGA and DDR, but the P1.1V net has a missing connection between the C52.1 component at (160.12, 97.40) and the U3 escape near (159.95, 98.15). VBUS has 38.75 mm of 0.40 mm track in aggregate, yet the J3.A4→U5.13 path takes 29.07 mm for endpoints only 5.1 mm apart. The nearby 1.13 mm charger-to-C77 connection helps locally. The long input path adds impedance upstream.

**Routing and manufacture · 3/10.** All 436 vias are through holes. Of these, 348 have a 0.15 mm drill, 80 have a 0.20 mm drill and eight have a 0.30 mm drill. Dominant track widths are 0.10–0.20 mm, with 343 segments at 0.089 mm. The bend screen found no acute two-segment corners away from pads and vias on runs of at least 0.10 mm. Drill-to-inner-track clearance is a larger issue, with 167 through vias below 0.20 mm, including the VBATT drill at (146.97, 101.72), 0.1545 mm from In2 `SD0_DAT1`. Saved and refilled DRC agree on 29 opens, 16 dangling tracks and one dangling via, the USB negative via at (138.25, 101.73). Three opens are on `FPGA_RESET`. The ordinary drill spans make a corrected board plausible, but this revision will not support normal bring-up.

## B — incomplete power, boot, USB and memory paths

**EMI · 2/10.** GND fills exist only on In1 and In3, covering 63.5% and 70.1%. In1 carries 146.9 mm of tracks, including 31.5 mm of `IO_9` and 16.3 mm of `ECP5_VREF`. The entire 3.16 mm F-side `REF_CLK` route misses In1 ground. At (166.62, 100.80) the clock projects within 0.035 mm of an In1 VREF track centerline, while the nearest In1 GND fill is 1.08 mm away. The 18.03 mm B-side USB positive run has power tracks on In4 and no adjacent ground plane. Its via at (136.21, 97.60) is 4.86 mm from the nearest GND through via and the track comes within 1.65 mm of the edge. There are 93 GND vias, one within 2 mm of the perimeter, yet DRC finds 44 missing GND connections. One involves R16.2 (138.96, 97.63) and the J3 shell component at (139.74, 98.63). Return continuity is compromised even at the cable entry.

**SI · 1/10.** Connector-side USB positive and negative total 38.64 and 28.51 mm, use different layer sequences, and have no sampled positive-leg run within 0.35 mm of the negative leg on the same layer. J3.A6/A7→R21/R29 measure 36.78/21.96 mm. The FPGA-side `USB_D+` is open between the U3.N1 escape at (159.45, 96.65) and the R20/R21 component. Both RAM clock polarities have two missing connections each, including U3.J18 (161.45, 105.15). `RAM_D11`, D12 and D15 are also open. Even the near-equal 20.47/20.20 mm lower-strobe lengths hide dissimilar paths, with two layer changes on positive and none on negative. Flash `SPI_CONFIG_SCK` has only 0.35 mm of routed track. U3.U16 (157.45, 104.15), U6.6 and R31 are in separate components. This blocks ordinary flash boot.

**Power · 1/10.** VBUS and VBATT use 0.50 mm tracks and most of the 3.3 V distribution is 0.30 mm, but VBUS, VBATT, VSYS, P1.1V, P1.35V, P2.5V and P3.3V all have missing connections. J3.A4 (140.56, 100.50) is separated from the In2 VBUS route near (143.84, 100.56). One VSYS gap separates the component containing C6.2 (151.03, 107.23) from the charger-side network. C6 still has a short local connection to U1.3. Its proximity cannot supply an isolated island. All three switch nodes are only 0.0889 mm wide with no reinforcing pour. U2.4 (148.96, 103.51) to L2.1 is 1.44 mm at that width. These are pulsed inductor-current paths on 2 A-class regulators. The traces and skinny local ground returns squander the fixed close placement. Six `ECP5_VREF` opens additionally undermine the memory interface.

**Routing and manufacture · 1/10.** Of 391 vias, 316 are through and 75 are 0.25/0.10 mm land/drill microvias from F to In1. The exported front–In1 drill file requires a blind-via process. A GND microvia at (162.20, 104.90) and a GND through via at (162.24, 104.85) have overlapping drills. KiCad reports a 0.000 mm hole gap. There are three hole-spacing warnings, 57 dangling tracks and 43 dangling vias. More than a thousand segments are only 0.0889 mm wide, including long USB runs outside the BGA. The bend screen found no isolated acute corners under the same test as D. The inner drill-clearance screen flags 119 through vias, including 0.156 mm from the GND drill at (143.35, 100.83) to VBUS. The project reports no ordinary track-clearance errors, but 120 missing connections remain after refill.

## Order decision and authorship

1. **A** has the shortest path to a standard order. Preserve its DDR grouping and power-loop geometry while correcting the refill, remaining DRC and fab clearances.
2. **C** is electrically complete and its KiCad refill is reproducible. Its stack of blind and buried drills needs an HDI quotation, and its clock-plane crossing and DQ timing spread need review.
3. **D** has an ordinary drill build and useful local power copper. USB negative, RAM reset and other essential connections require routing work.
4. **B** needs reconstruction of power and ground connectivity as well as the flash, memory and USB interfaces. Its microvias add process cost to an incomplete design.

**Human guess: A, 96% confidence.** The evidence is the coordination across interfaces. All sixteen DDR data nets use two vias, each byte stays primarily on a consistent layer, and the routed lengths cluster tightly. The same layout gives each buck its own small switch polygon and connects both sides of every input capacitor in local copper. It achieves this with through drills and no drill centers on either BGA's ball lands. C shows care on individual differential pairs, but the wider bus and the drill build do not show the same coordination. Authorship is an inference from the copper. The scores above stand independently of it.
