# Raspberry Pi CM4 NVMe NAS carrier layout review

**Hold the order. A is the best candidate to take through release.** It has complete connectivity, a credible impedance geometry on its stated stackup, and substantial power distribution. Its Ethernet pair 0 still needs work, and some of its M.2 solder lands contain drilled vias. C is the closest alternative. B has faults that would prevent assembly and operation.

Scores run from 1 to 10. Overall is a release judgment. Averaging category scores would understate the consequences of an open rail or an unbuildable placement.

## What was checked

I opened all four boards with KiCad 10.0.8, exported the common schematic netlist, and ran DRC on project copies both as saved and after zone refill. I also enabled the projects’ disabled courtyard, silk-on-copper and silk-overlap checks and independently screened with a 0.30 mm edge margin and 0.20 mm hole-to-copper margin. The 0.30 mm edge screen provides review margin above JLCPCB’s 0.20 mm routed-edge minimum.

The four outlines match and span (80.40, 54.55) to (227.80, 145.55) mm. All 254 references, footprint library IDs and 1,302 pad records agree across boards on pad number, net, shape, size, drill and local position. All 38 fixed footprints have the same position, angle and side. A, B and C retain all 38 locks. D retains only the Module1 lock. Placement is scored because the job left 216 footprints for each designer to place. The comparison is between the supplied projects. A separate original placement master and designer NOTES.md files were unavailable.

Coordinates below are KiCad millimetres, Y increasing downwards. Lengths are planar track centreline lengths including arcs. Via counts are separate. For branched nets I identify net totals or individual paths explicitly. Capacitor distances are pad centre to pad centre in XY. Opposite-side placement adds a vertical path. Ground coverage is the union of saved filled GND zones divided by board area. For a signal I also checked the copper directly under its centreline on the adjacent layer. A power island can carry a return over part of a route, but crossing its boundary requires a path to transfer that return.

All four projects report 17 footprint technology/land-pattern errors, including J1 at (113.30, 134.83), 196 footprint/library mismatch warnings, 24 library issue warnings and six silk-to-edge warnings. These require a footprint and assembly-output review. They are shared issues. The GND via count includes component ground connections as well as stitching. Likewise, a drill in an exposed thermal pad presents a different assembly problem from a drill in a socket contact or small chip-capacitor land.

**Shared schematic release issue.** Each project has 199 pad-net parity warnings and 16 symbol-field warnings. Many concern generated net names, such as C74.1 being called `Net-(C74-Pad1)` on the PCB and `Net-(U18-XIN)` in the schematic. There is also a substantive source ambiguity. KiCad warns of annotation errors on schematic export, and the exported netlist lists Module1.84 under **both** +1V8 and +3V3. On every PCB, Module1.84 at (92.78, 127.05) is +3V3 and Module1.88 at (92.78, 127.85) is +1V8. Reconcile the CM4 symbol units and pin map before using schematic parity as release evidence. The export discrepancy alone does not establish a copper short. The shells of J7 and J15 also carry separate `unconnected-(…-PadS1)` nets on all four boards. Verify the intended cable-shield/chassis discharge path for the product.

**Stackup and fab assumptions.** A specifies 0.11 / 1.22 / 0.11 mm dielectrics. B, C and D specify 0.48 / 0.48 / 0.48 mm. All specify 35 µm copper and dielectric constant 4.5. As a screening calculation, I solved a 2-D quasi-static odd-mode cross-section over a solid adjacent ground plane, with rectangular copper and air above. Using measured straight-run width / edge gap, A’s 0.17 / 0.15 mm USB geometry gives about **89 Ω differential** and its 0.18 / 0.14 mm PCIe geometry about **86 Ω**. The 0.20 / 0.20 mm USB geometry on C and D gives about **124 Ω** on their stated stack. The comparison targets are nominally 90 Ω for USB, 85 Ω for PCIe and 100 Ω for Ethernet. Solder mask, coplanar pours, foil shape, glass construction and the ordered fab stack will change these values. The actual JLC stack and finished dimensions need approval before plotting.

All four have a minimum drawn track width of 0.127 mm and minimum via drill of 0.20 mm. These dimensions are within JLCPCB’s published multilayer capability at 1 oz. Its guide specifies 0.20 mm from NPTH to track and from copper to routed board edge. It flags an extra-cost combination for 0.20 or 0.25 mm holes with via diameters below 0.45 mm. A standard four-layer quote also needs an explicit decision on filled and capped via-in-pad where solderable lands are drilled. ## A · Overall 8/10

**EMI · 8/10.** In1 has 93.7% GND fill and carries no tracks. The full CM4 USB pair and upstream PCIe clock run above ground. In2 is 60.9% GND with power regions. The slot-4 clock illustrates the effort put into bottom-side returns. At its P-leg transition (213.36, 59.96), the nearest GND via is 0.65 mm away. About 97% of its outer-layer route has adjacent GND. At the supply entry, J6.1 (117.65, 64.90) feeds a local +12P island, TVS IC1 and eFuse U3. The buck switch branches stay local. The exceptions worth testing are the front-side `ETH_P2+` only 0.54 mm from the left edge at (81.02, 97.98), and `PICO_PWM4` running 119.4 mm with six vias to the external fan header M4.4 (105.40, 88.74).

**Signal integrity · 8/10.** From L7 to Module1.105/.103, `USB_DP/DN` measure 60.4164 / 60.4163 mm, entirely on F.Cu. The median centre spacing is 0.32 mm. The upstream PCIe clock is 51.21 / 51.22 mm and three downstream clocks also stay on top. The slot TX lanes use AC capacitors near U12, and the copper mismatch on the two sides of C52/C53 largely cancels end to end. The clearest defect is `ETH_P0+/-` from Module1 through U9 to U7.R2/R3 near (100.15, 72.00). The PCB paths measure 56.22 / 60.86 mm, with three vias on P and zero on N. On J15, `USBpico_DP` alone changes sides twice near (219.52, 127.75). At J7, the D− contact at (111.05, 62.12) feeds a 2.44 mm copper extension to the alternate orientation contact at (110.05, 62.12). That extension is a stub when the cable uses the nearer contact. Y1 at (199.60, 125.10) has a 6.07 mm XIN net including its load capacitor, entirely on top. U18–U17 QSPI clock is 9.39 mm with no vias. The SD interface is less even, with `SD_DAT3` at 52.5 mm against a 36.4 mm clock.

**Power · 8/10.** Both the copper widths and the placement distinguish the pulsed inductor current from the small bootstrap current. U10.2 (182.26, 76.00) to L3.1 is a direct 1.91 mm × 0.42 mm run. The 0.20 mm branch of `Net-(C40-Pad2)` goes to bootstrap capacitor C40. The nearest 10 µF VIN capacitor C18 is 2.71 mm from U10.1, and its GND pad is 3.89 mm from U10.3. Those connections could be tighter. +12V and +5V both have inner-layer distribution in addition to tracks. At x = 145.30, the +5V pour spans y = 104.20–110.00, alongside a separate 0.60 mm track. U3’s +12V output serves seven bucks. The +12V width narrows materially by x = 187.30, where one inner crossing spans only 0.92 mm alongside a bottom track. Check simultaneous SSD load steps, fan startup and CM4 voltage at the far load.

**Placement · 8/10.** U12 (171.50, 83.00) is close to the sockets. Its +1V2 pin 9 and C22.1 are only 0.48 mm apart in projection, with the capacitor on the back. The repeated SSD converters and their inductors sit on the back, leaving the top card volume available. U17’s bypass C76 is 1.40 mm from pin 8 on the same side. The tradeoff is two-sided assembly with 185 back footprints, including all 16 status LEDs. D9 (104.20, 135.00) and D16 (123.10, 135.00) need a bottom viewing path in the enclosure.

**Routing and manufacture · 7/10.** There are no opens or routed-copper errors under the supplied rules after refill, and no reported courtyard overlaps. The 0.16 mm locating-hole waiver is restricted to pads within J7 or J15. The main production cost is 215 vias at 0.41 / 0.20 mm diameter/drill. A via also lands in J2.14 at (180.00, 66.15), and another in C17.1 at (92.25, 127.15). Move these out of the solder lands or specify and cost the appropriate fill/cap process. Small backtracking jogs remain, including a 0.041 mm leg with a 15° included angle on `TSENSE_2` at (184.08, 119.43). Silk over pads and the board legend crossing the U12 outline near (175.64, 87.61) need cleanup.

## B · Overall 1/10

**EMI · 2/10.** In1 has 93.7% GND coverage and the main USB trunk stays above it. That plane cannot serve pads left disconnected. KiCad reports 16 GND opens, including Module1.7 at (89.70, 111.85). Bottom-side `ETH_P0+` crosses an In2 +5V region for 12.3 mm around (102.73, 79.55). Only about 30% of its outer-layer copper has adjacent GND. A slot-4 clock via at (187.75, 112.14) is 6.36 mm from the nearest GND via. The saved In1 pour comes within 0.001 mm of the routed left edge at (80.40, 95.45). The oscillator also takes its high-impedance nodes off the component side.

**Signal integrity · 2/10.** J15’s D+ signal stops at R59. U18.47 (201.00, 114.56) and R59.2 (201.75, 111.31) are an explicit open. On the CM4 USB port, `USB_DP/DN` are 51.23 / 50.36 mm, but the 0.20 mm traces run at a median centre spacing of 1.57 mm. The long vertical sections at x = 110.55 and 112.12 behave largely as separate lines. Even the J7 D− contact tie uses two vias and a 1.00 mm bottom segment at y = 63.22. `PCIe_RX_P_1/N_1` measure 47.35 / 67.46 mm with two / four vias. The slot-4 clock differs by 10.56 mm. At U18 and Y1 (207.00, 124.00), XIN has two vias and the Y1.3/load-cap net has four. QSPI clock travels 17.64 mm with two vias and QSPI select reaches 29.13 mm. The matched upstream PCIe clock cannot compensate for missing downstream reset connections.

**Power · 1/10.** The layout reserves useful copper, including a broad bottom +12V zone and an inner +5V region, but KiCad reports eight +12V, seventeen +3.3VSTB and three +3.3V_M1 missing connections. These include the M1 rail to J2.70 (194.00, 66.15). The U10 switch pin and L3.1 are only 1.50 mm apart, but the entire routed switch net is just 0.127 mm wide on B.Cu. C18.1 is 0.75 mm from VIN while its return pad is 3.65 mm from U10.3. Close positive pads and a large plane do not complete the pulsed-current path.

**Placement · 2/10.** U3’s F.Fab body occupies x = 115.95–120.05, y = 70.95–75.05. The entire body of C5 at (116.58, 72.03) and most of C51 at (119.20, 72.33) lie inside U3 on F.Cu. U3 lacks a courtyard, so the courtyard run reports zero. This is a physical assembly blocker. Some switch decouplers are accurately located on the reverse side of U12, and the SSD converters are near their loads. By contrast, U17.8 (213.50, 107.25) is 11.19 mm in projection from even the nearest same-rail grounded capacitor, C84 on the opposite side.

**Routing and manufacture · 1/10.** The 147 missing connections persist after refill. There are also eight dangling vias and two dangling track reports, including +3.3VSTB at (202.60, 114.55). The board uses 485 vias, mostly 0.45 / 0.20 mm. A GND via at (212.25, 54.93) leaves just 0.154 mm to the edge. `PCIe_CLK_P_3` passes 0.0668 mm from J4’s NPTH at (150.00, 63.88), far inside the 0.20 mm NPTH-to-track clearance. A 17° turn on the U2 switch net at (171.26, 96.00) is a lower priority. There are 199 reported silk-on-copper and 147 silk-overlap findings. Completing this design requires component movement and substantial rerouting.

## C · Overall 7/10

**EMI · 7/10.** In1 is 92.6% GND and its nine track segments are themselves GND. In2 is 82.6% GND. The main USB and four Ethernet pairs remain on F.Cu, and the four downstream clock pairs stay over In1. The upstream clock takes a bottom excursion between vias at (159.60, 89.40) and (165.95, 90.25). Its P leg has a transition as far as 4.5 mm from a GND via. The +3V3 trace runs 42.47 mm along the left perimeter at x = 80.875 with 0.325 mm trace-to-edge clearance. U10’s switch-to-inductor path is 3.86 mm long. On the fan side, `PICO_PWM4` runs 124.55 mm with two vias to M4.4. These are more informative emission risks than the board-wide count of 390 GND vias alone.

**Signal integrity · 6/10.** The pair routing is disciplined. CM4 `USB_DP/DN` are 52.1651 / 52.1655 mm, entirely on top. All four Ethernet pairs have essentially matched total copper and zero vias. The measured CM4-to-U7 paths for P0 are both 45.31 mm. The slot-4 clock is 82.02 / 82.02 mm on top, and the two sections on either side of the slot-1 AC capacitors C52/C53 at (190.65, 73.60) and (189.65, 73.60) are closely matched. The release limitation is impedance. The 0.20 mm traces with 0.20 mm gaps are far from the USB target on the stated 0.48 mm outer dielectric. J15’s `USBpico_DP/DN` pad branches total 10.21 / 11.19 mm with no vias. At J7 the D− tie adds 3.00 mm of track around y = 61.00 between reversible contacts, forming an orientation-dependent stub. XIN and the flash clock are compact at 6.03 and 8.38 mm respectively, both via-free. `SD_CMD` is an outlier at 71.43 mm and six vias from Module1.62 (92.78, 122.65) to J1.3 (114.00, 141.19), against a 32.30 mm SD clock.

**Power · 7/10.** J6, IC1, C5 and U3 share a 41 mm² top-side +12P fill. J6.1 is at (117.65, 64.90) and U3 is at (119.30, 75.20). A four-millimetre +12V inner strip crosses x = 145.30 and 187.30 at y = 76.40–80.40. The four SSD rails each have roughly 139 mm² of bottom pour. At U10, both the input and return pad distances to C1 are 1.91 mm. The 0.30-to-0.45 mm U10.2–L3.1 switch route could be shorter. The fan +5V distribution includes a single 77 mm × 0.60 mm bottom section from (128.00, 99.60) to (205.00, 99.60). At 35 µm copper that section alone is about 63 mΩ at room temperature, before vias and return resistance. Size it against the combined downstream fan-start current.

**Placement · 8/10.** U12 at (171.50, 87.50) sits between the module and sockets. U10, U11, U13 and U14 repeat at socket pitch on the back. The inductors stay out of the installed SSD volume. U18.10 (206.50, 120.06) has C82.1 just 0.49 mm away in projection on the back, and C76.1 is 1.13 mm from U17.8 on top. Unlike A, all 16 LEDs are on the top, including D9 at (84.60, 97.20), where they can be viewed from the component side. The assembly still requires both sides, with 109 back footprints. There are zero reported courtyard overlaps.

**Routing and manufacture · 6/10.** Zero opens after refill and fewer awkward corners than A. One 45° included-angle jog on `PCIe_RX_P_2` at (165.45, 75.97) has a 0.033 mm leg. The 755 vias use 0.45 / 0.20, 0.5032 / 0.30 and 0.60 / 0.30 mm geometries. Fourteen via centres land on same-net SMD copper, including C11.2 at (117.90, 82.05). The project’s zero hole-clearance minimum hides the decisive DFM fault. Both `Net-(R14-Pad1)` on the back and `Net-(R15-Pad1)` on the front pass just 0.0501 mm from U7’s locating hole at (89.985, 65.65). Move those routes before release. The extra checks also report 59 silk-on-copper and 21 silk overlaps, including the fixed legend across U12 near (166.89, 82.89).

## D · Overall 6/10

**EMI · 6/10.** D has the densest stitching, with 802 GND vias, and useful local returns. The slot-4 clock changes sides at (213.33, 60.25), only 0.66 mm from a GND via. In1 and In2 have 90.7% and 81.1% GND coverage as saved. Two local discontinuities matter. `ETH_P0+` traverses a 4.37 mm stretch above an In1 void near (99.70, 77.56), where the plane edge is about 2.19 mm away. Bottom-side `PCIe_RX_P_3` runs over +3.3VA for 4.33 mm around (163.65, 126.46). The top `LED_V+` trace follows the edge for 20.65 mm at y = 55.15 with 0.475 mm clearance. `PICO_PWM4` travels 166 mm and three vias to M4.4. The local stitching does more for the fast pairs than for this long cable-driving signal.

**Signal integrity · 5/10.** CM4 USB is 51.13 / 51.09 mm with zero vias over ground. QSPI clock is only 3.90 mm between U18.52 and U17.6, and XIN stays on top. J15’s P/N nets have 4.89 / 4.06 mm of copper on the connector side of R59/R60 and 7.21 / 6.71 mm on the MCU side. J7’s D− reversible-contact tie adds a 2.76 mm stub when driven through the nearer contact. The PCIe lanes are generally close and via-paired. One clear exception controls the score. From J4.41/.43 to U12.41/.42, `PCIe_RX_P_3/N_3` are **101.18 / 109.98 mm**, two vias each. The N leg has a dense trombone near (160, 129). The residual 8.79 mm mismatch is material at PCIe edge rates. The slot-4 clock is well matched at 106.29 mm, but is long and predominantly 0.127 mm wide over the specified 0.48 mm dielectric. The model gives about 134 Ω for the representative 0.127 / 0.173 mm geometry of the slot-3 RX pair. Ethernet P0 also differs by 3.29 mm between module and jack.

**Power · 8/10.** D gives the high-current paths real copper area. At x = 187.30 the top +12V trunk spans y = 81.40–86.90, and most of the +5V distribution is 0.80 mm wide. Each buck has a shaped top SW pour of about 4 mm². U10.2 and L3.1 are 2.45 mm apart. C18.1 is 1.51 mm from U10 VIN, while its GND pad is 3.26 mm from U10 GND. The bootstrap branch deserves revision. The same fast SW node goes down at (195.70, 76.15), travels 6.65 mm on the bottom, and comes up at (189.65, 75.60) to reach C40. That excursion injects switching noise even though the main inductor current can stay in the top pour. The 0.20 mm M.2 pin tracks also depend on the local pours to carry slot current.

**Placement · 7/10.** All 254 footprints are on the front, simplifying assembly and inspection. The status LEDs are on the visible side, U17.8 is only 1.10 mm from C76.1, and the four drive regulators repeat beside their sockets. The switch U12 at (173.30, 123.45) is about 57 mm below the M.2 connector origins before routing detours. Its +1V2 bypass at U12.9 is 3.29 mm away on the same side. Top-side inductors, including L2 at (166.50, 98.30), occupy the plan area of an installed SSD. Check height against the socket, drive underside and standoff. Nine courtyard overlaps include C9/C13 near (121, 85) and IC1/TP_VIN1 near (118, 72). Verify probe and rework access as well as body clearance.

**Routing and manufacture · 5/10.** D has no opens. Its saved fill has 40 zone-to-zone clearance errors that disappear after refill. Nineteen equal-priority zone intersections persist, all between zones on the same net. Examples are the +3.3V_M2 outlines at (173.30, 72.80) and (172.20, 64.65). The intersecting same-net boundaries complicate reproducible fills. KiCad reports no shorts. At the left edge (80.40, 95.45), In1 GND pulls back 0.20 mm as saved and only 0.05 mm after a refill with the original rules. Establish the edge rule before regenerating Gerbers. Of 1,049 vias, 1,035 use 0.45 / 0.20 mm. Many ground drills are useful thermal connections at U12, but two vias also land in C73.2 near (88, 81.6). Most bends are conventional or rounded. A small 45° included-angle +5V jog at (214.35, 109.81) is secondary to the refill and assembly issues. D has the fewest reported silk-on-copper findings, nine, and eight silk overlaps.

## Release choice and authorship

1. **A** has the best combination of actual pair geometry, local return paths and broad rail distribution. Resolve Ethernet P0, choose the via-in-pad process, and confirm the stack and common schematic pin map before ordering it.
2. **C** has the most consistent Ethernet pairing and good converter placement. Its stackup mismatch, RJ45 drill clearance and long SD command route put it behind A.
3. **D** has strong current distribution, closely coupled PCIe routing and one-sided assembly. Its slot-3 RX skew, longer channels and changes in copper on refill outweigh those advantages.
4. **B** needs a new placement around U3 and completion of basic power, ground, reset and USB connectivity before an SI or fab sign-off is meaningful.

**Human guess — D, 97% confidence.** The combination of one-sided assembly, individually shaped copper at the seven bucks, locally placed thermal and return vias, and selective arc-based length tuning is distinctive. The placement also favours visible indicators and repeated, serviceable power blocks even at the cost of longer lanes. This is an inference from the design choices. The ordering judgment above gives full weight to the resulting skew and fabrication defects.
