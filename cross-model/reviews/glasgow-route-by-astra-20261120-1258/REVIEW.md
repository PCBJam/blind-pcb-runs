# Glasgow four-layer layout review

**Select A for release work. Hold the order.** A has the best combination of FPGA bus routing, predictable return paths, and low-impedance power distribution. Its two most immediate fabrication defects are measurable and localized in scope. Six pours approach the routed edge within 0.051 mm, and two via pairs have only 0.150 mm between drill walls. B is the runner-up and needs fewer changes to its copper clearances.

**Shared release items.** All four produced the same 421 schematic-parity warnings, including 199 pad-net naming conflicts. I compared the exported schematic netlist by component and pin. The named electrical pad memberships agree up to net renaming, including PCB `+3V3` versus schematic `+3.3V`. This distinguishes assigned nets from physical continuity, which fails on C. Reconcile the assembly BOM for C90 at (127.0, 105.7) and C91 at (119.6, 85.3). Their board value fields are “C90” and “C91” while the schematic specifies 1 nF. The shared artwork has 26 silk-over-mask, 31 silk-overlap, six silk-at-edge and 32 text-height warnings. The “B2” legend at (94.50, 99.70) clips against U23.2 and J1 silk clips at the left edge. Review the plotted legend and mechanical courtyards before assembly.

**Fabrication basis.** I used JLCPCB’s published 1 oz multilayer capabilities for a routed profile. Its listed limits include 0.09/0.09 mm trace/space, 0.20 mm copper to routed edge and 0.20 mm via hole-to-hole. It identifies a 0.20 mm drill with a land below 0.45 mm as an extra-cost selection. Every candidate uses tracks of at least 0.15 mm. The fixed SW1.3 shield pad at (74.65, 119.23) measures **0.170 mm** from the edge in the 0.20 mm DRC on every board. Obtain the fab’s disposition on that pad. Only A also fails this check on added zones. [JLCPCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)

### Critical signal measurements

The high-speed paths are the 480 Mbit/s USB interface from J1 through the U15 protection area to U1, the FX2-to-FPGA FIFO and clocks, and 13 expansion pairs from U30 to J5. The schematic puts LVDS termination on the daughterboard. U2 and U3 are I²C EEPROMs on the shared `/SCL` and `/SDA` bus. U8 and U36 are 3.3 V / 500 mA and 1.2 V / 300 mA **linear** regulators. U14 and U31 are adjustable bank LDOs. The relevant fast current loops are USB protection, regulator bypassing, FPGA supply transients, and simultaneous I/O switching.

The following are approximate planar centreline paths in mm, measured from **each** reversible J1 contact to U1.8 or U1.9. They include the connector straps, follow contacts through pads where necessary, and omit vertical barrel travel. The last column counts layer transitions on the A-contact path. Adding all the copper on each USB net would mix branches and test points into the skew measurement.

For plane coverage I intersected trace centrelines with the actual adjacent-layer fill polygons. C coverage below uses its refilled copper. A gap indicates that the nearest plane cannot carry the image current directly below that span. Other pours, tracks, paired conductors, and nearby vias can still carry return current.

**Impedance matters independently of skew.** On the long straight USB sections the measured width/gap is 0.19/0.15 mm on A, 0.20/0.15 on B, 0.19/0.26 on C, and 0.30/0.21 on D. A–C declare 0.48 mm from outer copper to the nearest plane. D declares 0.215 mm. For scale, a 2-D odd-mode electrostatic estimate with the file dielectric, 35 µm rectangular traces, a solid plane, and air above gives roughly **115, 114, 136, and 93 Ω**, respectively, against a 90 Ω USB target. These estimates omit solder mask, nearby coplanar copper, etch shape, pads, and vias. JLC’s listed 1.6 mm JLC04161H-7628 build uses 0.2104 mm outer dielectric and 0.0152 mm inner copper. The order needs an agreed stack and fabricator-calculated USB and LVDS geometry. The bare-board geometry review does not establish an eye diagram or EMC compliance. ## A — best electrical layout, two fabrication limits missed

- **EMI · 8/10.** L2 has a single 3,433 mm² GND fill with no signal tracks. Separate `/SHLD` copper around J1 collects shell current on all four layers, with the shield-to-ground network at C13/R3 near (59, 92) and (59, 99). The shield split costs some reference at the connector straps, including 1.59 mm of top `/USB_P` over L2 shield. Both crystal nets have GND underneath their entire top-layer length. Switching data is kept away from the perimeter. The nearest ordinary signal is `/ENVB` at (72.60, 118.25), 1.675 mm copper-to-edge. The weakness is bottom routing over L3 power. At (77.56, 99.34), 2.23 mm of a `/D0` segment has no L3 fill beneath it. The `/CLKREF` via at (78.60, 94.40) is 3.96 mm from the nearest GND via or ground PTH centre. A has 121 GND vias, concentrated on circuits, with none within 2 mm of the edge.
- **SI · 8/10.** The main USB pair stays on top through U15. At x = 60 its centrelines are y = 95.310 and 95.650. A-contact skew is about 0.05 mm. The B-contact path adds two transitions to each leg and the longer positive strap increases planar skew to 2.31 mm. The standout is J5. All 26 `/IO_Banks/Z0…Z12` conductors measure 28.52–29.60 mm. Twelve pairs match within 0.05 mm, and layer changes are paired. Z0 is the exception at 29.60/28.95 mm between the U30.B1/B2 area and J5.39/41 near (83.3, 78.4). The routed `/D0…/D7` range is 11.45–18.68 mm. Y1’s `/XTALIN` and `/XTALOUT` are 8.25/6.38 mm including their capacitor branches, both via-free. `/CLKREF` is less direct, with 36.07 mm of tree copper, four vias, and a 1.45 mm TP6 spur from (73.10, 90.45) to (73.10, 89.00). The declared stack limits the USB impedance score.
- **Power · 9/10.** FB1.2 at (59.535, 98.20) feeds U15.3 through 0.45 mm copper. The 0.15 mm `/VUSB` branch also visible here goes to the enable pin. U15.4 at (63.00, 98.25) launches into 0.75 mm `+5V`. Of 136.91 mm total on this rail, 113.95 mm is 0.75 mm wide, including the 25.60 mm L3 run at y = 118.20. U8.6 and input C26.2 are joined on top with 0.35 mm copper. U8.1 at (66.95, 115.65) feeds C28.1 at (68.50, 116.19) through a short 0.35 mm link reinforced by a local pour. U36 repeats this geometry for C27. Local GND copper and plated exposed pads keep the capacitor returns close. Both adjustable bank regulators have local output and shunt pours, including U31.5 at (122.20, 90.75). The FPGA core uses a smaller 18.1 mm² L3 island beneath U30 with a broad feed. At the FPGA, U30.D6 and backside decoupler C21.1 share the same supply via at (85.70, 95.90), with 0.57 and 0.62 mm from ball and capacitor centres to the via.
- **Routing / fabrication · 6/10.** Most vias are roomy 0.60/0.30 mm land/drill, 364 of 416. The 46 smaller 0.40/0.20 mm vias require the fab’s corresponding drill option. Six pours fail the fab edge check, including L2 GND at (50.05, 76.00) and `/SHLD` at (50.05, 95.00). Of 16 drill-spacing warnings, two are below JLC’s 0.20 mm via-to-via figure. On `VIOB`, the 0.30 mm drills at (97.05, 106.95) and (97.50, 106.95) leave only 0.15 mm. The second pair is at (107.30, 105.40) and (107.75, 105.40). U8’s fan-out contains overlapping 0.75 mm segments at C28.1. The untied barrel on R40.1 at (70.608, 113.308) also consumes a solder land. Of eight via centres inside SMD lands, six are on test points and two are on R40. Filling and capping are disabled. Pull the pours back, separate the drills, and clean up R40 before releasing Gerbers.

## B — strong power layout, weaker USB transitions

- **EMI · 7/10.** The 3,385 mm² L2 ground is free of routed tracks. Outer GND pours and 165 GND vias give returns several ways to change layers. A 130.5 mm² L3 GND patch under J1/U15 supports the bottom-routed USB pair. It lies under 6.07 of 7.71 mm of bottom P and 7.14 of 7.74 mm of bottom N. The return transition near U15 is less compact. From the signal vias at (62.15, 96.35) and (63.90, 97.30), the nearest GND via or PTH centres are 2.29 and 2.67 mm away. L3 power splits also matter at the FPGA. The entire 4.78 mm bottom `/D0` segment at x = 78.90, y = 98.12–102.90 is over a void. `/ENVB` runs 47.92 mm along the top edge at y = 71.50 with 0.425 mm clearance. Its low toggle rate makes this less serious than routing a clock there.
- **SI · 7/10.** USB planar matching is good in either orientation, but both main conductors change layers twice. The two bottom straights run alongside each other at a 0.15 mm gap for 2.75 mm near (60, 95.75). They separate at the launches. The P and N vias by U15 are almost 2 mm apart and U15.5 connects by a 0.90 mm side branch from the N via. The 24 MHz crystal routing is cleaner, 7.07/5.98 mm, entirely on top over GND. `/CLKREF` is a 25.15 mm tree with four vias and passes through TP6. `/CLKIF` is 23.39 mm with two vias. Five FIFO data lines stay on top and the bus spans 11.85–24.21 mm. J5 pairs generally run together, with up to about 1.00 mm intra-pair mismatch. Z5 is 21.53/22.53 mm at U30.E2/E3, around (84.5, 93). The 14.12–28.54 mm spread across lanes leaves less timing margin for a source-synchronous daughterboard than A provides. No inter-lane skew limit is specified.
- **Power · 9/10.** U15.4 launches into 0.80 mm `+5V` at (63.00, 98.25). A 126.8 mm² L3 strip near y = 118 carries the rail across the board, with a 0.60 mm bottom branch from (119.90, 118.40) to (119.90, 101.50). Separate L3 VIOA/VIOB regions serve the switching translators. The 51.3 mm² `+1V2` region is continuous from the U36 output vias at (69.50, 110.70/111.50) to the FPGA capacitor vias. U8.1 to C28.1 is a direct 1.64 mm by 0.50 mm top trace. The output then fans into two 0.40 mm links. C28.2 has a GND via 0.81 mm away and joins the front ground pour. B also places a return via within 0.60 mm of each Y1 load-capacitor ground pad. At U30.D6 a 0.57 mm escape lands directly in the backside C21.1 land. This shortens the supply side of the FPGA bypass loop at the assembly cost discussed below.
- **Routing / fabrication · 7/10.** No opens, dangling copper, or additional native board DRC violations beyond the shared items. Pours stop about 0.40 mm from the edge. Of 475 vias, 384 are 0.45/0.20 mm and 23 are 0.40/0.20 mm. Assembly is the main issue. **29** via centres lie inside same-net SMD lands, including U5.15 at (110.20, 100.425) and C21.1 at (85.70, 95.10), with filling and capping disabled. Solder can wick into a drill inside a pad opening even with general via tenting enabled. Move the affected vias or specify a suitable pad process. A folded 45° interior corner on bank-A buffer Z3 at (102.25, 80.20) is a minor cleanup beside the assembly risk.

## C — electrical completion failure

- **EMI · 3/10.** After refill L2 has a contiguous 3,479 mm² GND zone and no signal tracks. U15.2 at (60.80, 97.30) has a ground via 0.78 mm away. The other side is much worse. L3 contains five overlapping outlines assigned to `+3V3`, with large voids after refill and no GND region for bottom-layer returns. Of 24.51 mm of bottom `/IO_Banks/Z5_N`, none lies over filled L3 copper. One continuous uncovered segment is 12.94 mm, centred at (79.92, 76.72). Its P mate stays on top. There are 154 GND vias, but no outer ground fills to stitch together. `/ENVA` runs 45.10 mm along y = 71.64, only 0.565 mm from the edge, including 21.10 mm without adjacent L3 fill. The USB input also takes both sides of the board around (54.49, 90.16), enlarging the connector-to-bead path.
- **SI · 2/10.** The A-contact USB path reaches U1 on top, but the 0.26 mm straight gap is relatively wide on the declared stack. N alone necks to 0.15 mm for the final 2.92 mm from the U15 area. TP15 at (55.50, 96.30) is electrically open on P. Y1 traces are reasonably short at 7.32/5.87 mm, but have dangling through vias at (64.735, 94.25) and (64.385, 93.75), adding stubs directly on the oscillator nodes. `/CLKREF` misses U30.D5 at (85.30, 94.70). `/CLKIF` and FIFO `/D1`, `/D5`, `/D6`, and `/D7` also have missing connections. `/IO_Banks/Z7_P` has zero track between U30.F4 (83.70, 93.90) and J5.22 (71.865, 74.625). Both EEPROM clock pins U2.6 and U3.6 near (76.73, 108.04) and (73.23, 108.04) are on the open `/SCL` network. The short route fragments on these nets cannot be used as timing lengths.
- **Power · 2/10.** J1’s `/xVBUS` uses 16.82 mm of 0.15 mm copper and two vias. The entire 102.60 mm `+5V` tree uses 0.25 mm tracks, including a 12.20 mm bottom segment from (118.80, 100.99) to (106.60, 100.99). These dimensions add avoidable resistance ahead of bank loads. C26 reaches U8.6 with a short top trace, and C28.2 has a GND via only 0.675 mm away. The U8 output and C28 positive terminal reach `+3V3` by separate vias. More seriously, U14.5 at (124.40, 100.25) is disconnected from the R6/R7 output network. Both banks have opens in `ISNS_H`, including the bank-A connection between R56.2 (122.55, 89.61) and R49.1 (122.55, 88.39). The variable rails and current measurement cannot be relied on.
- **Routing / fabrication · 1/10.** Saved fills produce 335 clearance and 199 hole-clearance errors. One reports **zero** clearance between the C58.2-net via at (123.40, 82.60) and L2 GND. Refill clears these 534 errors. It leaves **153 open connections**, 45 dangling tracks, 25 dangling vias and four intersections between priority-zero `+3V3` zones on L3, including the outlines anchored at (89.60, 71.30) and (89.60, 75.00). The vias are reasonable sizes, 400 at 0.45/0.20 mm and 29 at 0.50/0.25 mm. No via centre lands inside an SMD land. Those choices cannot rescue incomplete copper. C needs routing, a fresh fill, and a new electrical review.

## D — credible differential geometry, excessive layer changes elsewhere

- **EMI · 5/10.** The design reserves a 528.5 mm² L3 GND region under the expansion fan-out. It covers 23.37 of 26.06 mm of bottom `Z12_P`. Outer pours and 148 GND vias help near the banks. The tradeoff is visible beside U30. There are 81.85 mm of tracks on L2 itself. The clearance slot around L2 `/D0` crosses the reference directly under top `/D4` at (78.70, 98.64) and `/D6` at (79.10, 97.00). An L2 `+1V2` trace similarly passes below top `Z8_N` at (83.00, 92.20). On the bottom, only about 0.39 of 6.45 mm of `/CLKREF` is over any L3 fill. A 3.09 mm uncovered segment is centred at (75.16, 91.26). These are return-path interruptions at the switching loads. The edge route is lower risk, though `/SDA` follows y = 119.60 for 11.80 mm with just 0.325 mm copper-to-edge.
- **SI · 7/10.** D has the closest nominal USB impedance, top-layer A paths, and a real rule keeping outer GND pours 0.55 mm away from USB and expansion conductors. At x = 60 the USB centrelines are y = 95.12 and 95.63. The positive test-point spur includes about 0.81 mm of bottom copper from the via at (54.92, 95.75). On the B-contact path, N crosses layers twice while P stays on top. Planar mismatch is 1.69 mm before adding barrel delay. For J5, a 0.22/0.18 mm paired section on the declared stack models near 103 Ω, though the actual gap opens in fan-outs. Z10 measures 25.68/26.70 mm. Y1 is good at 7.07/5.39 mm, via-free over GND. The FIFO is the limiting circuit. `/D0` uses **nine through vias** and 38.28 mm over all four layers, versus 12.25 mm for `/D4`. `/CLKIF` has four vias and `/CLKREF` five, including the transition at (78.65, 94.80), 3.77 mm from a GND via or PTH centre. Controlling the USB cross-section does little for this clock-to-data disparity.
- **Power · 6/10.** D has useful L3 copper for `+5V` and both bank rails, including a continuous 223.9 mm² `+5V` region reached from U15.4 by a via at (63.00, 98.825). C28.2 returns through both front GND and a nearby via at (68.50, 113.94). But U8.1 and C28.1 go to separate power vias at (66.95, 116.075) and (68.50, 116.863). This adds a layer excursion to a capacitor only 1.64 mm from the output pin. The `+1V2` fill breaks into five patches. The 33.0 mm² patch at U36 ends near (77.02, 102.95), separate from the FPGA patches. Other copper completes the net with 146.99 mm of routed tree and 21 vias, mostly 0.25 mm tracks. That total includes branches, but the broken plane and via-dependent paths still raise supply impedance. The local U31 feedback network is much better, only 3.41 mm of top copper including the divider branches at (120.90, 89.28).
- **Routing / fabrication · 6/10.** D has zero active board errors and zero opens under its own rules. It documents its four mixed-pad exclusions and sets a 0.25 mm routed-copper edge rule. The 672 through vias include 252 at 0.40/0.20 mm. Forty distinct via centres lie inside SMD lands, some under lands on both faces. Examples include U30.D9 at (85.325, 97.85) and U1.32/C4.1 at (73.625, 97.25). D **does** specify filling and capping. Carry that requirement onto the four-layer fab order and quote it, particularly for the BGA. Ordinary tenting is insufficient there. A 29° interior GND bend near (82.58, 94.38) and a 45° fold on `/D7` at (72.08, 101.02) are secondary to the excess drilling and return-plane cuts.

## Ranking and authorship

**A → B → D → C.** A’s tight LVDS matching, relatively direct FIFO and deliberate regulator copper justify fixing its drill and edge violations. B offers equally convincing power delivery and fewer copper-clearance changes, but needs USB transition and via-in-pad assembly review. D’s USB cross-section is the strongest individual high-speed feature. Its FPGA clock, data, and core-supply routing would require more extensive changes. C cannot operate as drawn.

**I think A is the human layout, with 98% confidence.** The strongest clue is how differently A treats three circuits on the same board. It tunes nearly every J5 conductor toward a common length, repeats the regulator-to-capacitor and shunt copper across equivalent circuits, and gives the connector shell its own shaped copper. That combination of bus-wide tuning and circuit-specific copper is more distinctive than a via count or a clean DRC report. The guess is based on geometry alone.
