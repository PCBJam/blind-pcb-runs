Overall scores express readiness to build a working, mountable adapter. A mechanical blocker can outweigh several high electrical scores.

## What the circuit requires

J2 is the M-key M.2 edge and J1 is an edge-mounted PCIe x4 socket. The high-speed channel consists of four `/Data_*_IN` pairs, four `/Data_*_OUT` pairs and `/REFCLK±`. All start on J2's back. The IN pairs and clock finish on J1's back, OUT on its front. There is no USB pair, crystal, flash bus or onboard regulator. The switching load of interest is the attached card. J2 supplies `/+3V3`, R1 links it to `/+3V3_AUX`, J3.1 and J4 share the slot's `+12V`, and `+5V` runs only between J3.4 and spring contact J5. J3.2 and J3.3 provide the supply returns.

Placement is **null for all four**. I compared all 20 footprint identities, positions, orientations and sides across the projects. All agree. The socket straddles the long lower edge and the power connector sits on the reverse at (175.59, 96.14). The fixed C2.1-to-J1.A3 distance is 4.80 mm and C1.1-to-J1.A3 is 6.45 mm, measured between pad centres. The copper and vias connecting those pads determine how much of that distance becomes a high-inductance loop.

## Measurements and common order conditions

Coordinates are KiCad board coordinates in mm, viewed from the front, with Y increasing downward. I inspected the schematic, rendered both routing faces, measured track geometry against saved fill polygons and Edge.Cuts, and exported plated and non-plated drills. I ran KiCad 10.0.8 DRC with schematic parity and exclusions on project copies, first as saved and again after refilling. Both runs gave the same findings on each board. Every board had zero parity errors and 20 footprint-library warnings. The Antmicro footprint library is unavailable for comparison with the embedded footprints. None of the four project directories includes the requested designer's NOTES.md.

Lengths below are sums of routed centrelines, including arcs, excluding vertical barrels and connector internals. Track endpoints inside a land vary by design. Same-layer gaps were sampled along the positive conductor at intervals of at most 0.05 mm. Via distances are centre-to-centre. A reference void means the projected signal centreline leaves GND copper on the adjacent layer. A small antipad, a deliberate pad relief and a long plane slot have different consequences.

**Impedance and fabrication.** All four files specify 0.876 mm overall, 0.120 mm from each outer signal layer to its adjacent inner layer, dielectric constant 4.2 there, 18 µm outer copper and 35 µm inner copper. For a long uniform microstrip over solid GND, a 2D quasi-static odd-mode estimate gives about **85 Ω** for A and **92 Ω** for B/C on the file stack. I used rectangular conductors and 10 µm mask with assumed dielectric constant 3.8. Changing only the finished outer copper to 35 µm gives approximately 81 Ω and 87 Ω respectively. These estimates exclude launches, etch shape, nearby side copper and connectors. D has too little uniform geometry for one number to describe its channels. Order against an agreed controlled-impedance stack and confirm the data and REFCLK targets with the host specification. A PCIe generation and card current budget are unspecified. No channel, load or thermal test was supplied.

**Standard JLCPCB fit.** The trace widths and 0.25–0.35 mm ordinary via drills are practical. The edge connector is the special-order item. JLC lists 1 oz finished outer copper for standard multilayer construction and ±0.1 mm thickness tolerance at 0.8 mm. Confirm mating thickness at J2 and the J1 straddle mount, finger finish and any required bevel on the drawing. A declares ENIG and an edge connector. B/C/D leave finish unspecified. The shared 0.7 mm references are below JLC's advertised 1.0 mm text height. A separate legend check on copies, at 1.0 mm height and 0.15 mm clearance, found 17 small texts and three close silk features on each board. C8's reference at (142.40, 109.45) clears C3's by only 0.104 mm. Project DRC uses zero minimum silk clearance. ## A — first, 9/10

- **EMI 8/10.** In2 is a near-board-wide GND reference. In1 shares GND with power, but the top PCIe routes stay in the GND corridor apart from local via clearances. At the `/Data_0_OUT_P` change at (132.98, 111.10), a GND via at (133.50, 110.10) joins both references 1.13 mm away. The M.2 launch is less tight. J2.39 GND at (107.65, 98.77) reaches its via at (111.75, 98.77), 4.10 mm from pad centre. The split affects sidebands more than lanes. Of the 15.69 mm front run of `/PWRGD`, 7.94 mm lacks adjacent GND, including 3.3 V copper beneath (112.22, 101.17). At (160, 104), the two 12 V pours are adjacent to one another and the first GND beneath them is In2. The nearest fast trace to the outline is `/REFCLK+`, about 1.19 mm from the key near (109.10, 102.77).
- **Signal integrity 9/10.** All nine pairs match to better than 0.001 mm within each pair. Data tracks run 36.26–37.35 mm and REFCLK runs 37.97 mm. Lane 0 IN follows a coupled diagonal from about (112.82, 99.70) to (121.80, 108.68). The 0.218 mm width is enforced by a project rule and the long coupled sections fit the nominal 85 Ω target well. The four OUT pairs change faces once per conductor. The IN pairs and clock change twice, adding two discontinuities even though their endpoints share a face. Coupling opens at the transitions and 1 mm-pitch socket lands. On lane 0 IN, 7.57 mm of the positive route, including pad runs, has more than 0.25 mm edge gap. The fast routes are point-to-point with no branch stubs.
- **Power 9/10.** At x = 160 the 12 V path is 3.57 mm wide on **each** of F and In1, from y = 101.98 to 105.55. Six 0.75/0.35 mm vias distribute current near the slot at x = 146.21–148.21, y = 114.65–115.55. The 3.3 V distribution has 19 vias, a broad In1 region, and 0.68 mm slot feeds. J3.4–J5 uses 1.4 mm copper. The C4 100 nF capacitor has local supply and GND vias each 0.69 mm from its pad centre at (141.13, 111.20) and (143.47, 111.20). C1 has equivalent distances of 0.90 and 0.71 mm. These local connections limit the loop penalty of the fixed capacitor bank.
- **Routing and manufacturability 8/10.** Zero open connections. The minimum routed width is 0.20 mm, and the 28 signal vias are ordinary 0.50/0.25 mm through holes. Bends are predominantly 45° changes, with no severe acute two-track corners. The 4.20 mm copper land with 3.50 mm drill at (186.207, 98.760) creates the retention half-hole. A coordinate-specific rule allows it through the 0.20 mm edge clearance. This is a sensible local exception, but the fab drawing still needs to identify the cut-through plated hole and required exposed land. The shared legend issue also applies.

## B — second, 8/10

- **EMI 9/10.** Both inner layers carry approximately 2,100 mm² of GND each across a 2,206 mm² outline. No signal tracks cut either plane. F/B layer changes see local antipads and nearby GND stitching. The `/REFCLK+` via at (135.54, 104.72) has a GND via 0.72 mm away at (136.24, 104.87). J2.39 reaches GND at (110.20, 98.77), 2.55 mm from pad centre. The front 12 V corridor around (160, 107) lies immediately over In1 GND. The clock's closest approach to the key is 1.21 mm. The long socket land runs and their fanout remain the main return discontinuities.
- **Signal integrity 8/10.** All four IN pairs and REFCLK measure about 44.43 mm per conductor, the OUT pairs about 45.27 mm. Maximum intra-pair track skew is below 0.001 mm. The 0.84 mm IN/OUT difference also closely offsets the extra full-board traversal on IN. The 0.185/0.145 mm coupled geometry is consistent, including through rounded tuning sections around (131, 106.8). There are 216 routed arcs. The tuning adds length and adjacent folded sections, and the file-stack estimate is about 92 Ω. Each polarity takes the same number of through vias as its mate. No fast-net branch stub was evident.
- **Power 6/10.** The front 12 V pour is 3.75 mm wide at x = 160, and the short J3-end feeds for 12 V and 5 V use 1.5 mm tracks. At the slot, the pour gives way to 0.40 mm traces up to 8.64 mm long. There are two 12 V vias. The larger concern is `/+3V3`. A broad M.2-end pour feeds narrow paths, including a 15.67 mm × 0.25 mm back trace from (139.21, 103.14) to J1.A10 at (139.21, 118.81). Several slot feeds share the load, but their individual resistance matters. C1.2 has a good local return 0.68 mm away at (145.23, 113.05). C4.1 reaches the nearest 3.3 V via only after a 3.91 mm straight-line separation, and C2.1's nearest 12 V via is 3.86 mm away. I would want the actual card load before accepting the slot voltage margin.
- **Routing and manufacturability 8/10.** The 92 through vias are the fewest here, with 0.55/0.25 and 0.60/0.30 mm standard sizes. The active electrical checks pass. The all-severity report includes **one excluded edge error** on the intentional 5.50/3.50 mm GND half-hole at (186.190, 98.760). Specify that operation and the finger finish at order time. The differential tuning is smooth. There is some redundant power copper, including a 0.25 mm segment that doubles back along a 0.40 mm segment at (146.01, 110.17). That is minor compared with the 3.3 V necks and the shared small legend.

## C — third, 6/10

- **EMI 8/10.** The top routes reference In1 GND. In2 shares GND with 3.3 V and 12 V while retaining GND along the back-side lane corridor. Of 189 vias, 115 are GND. The lane 0 OUT transition at (133.01, 105.82) has a GND via 0.95 mm away at (133.31, 104.92). J2.39 has a GND via only 2.30 mm from its centre. The main interruptions are at the launches. The In2 finger void from (106.00, 89.95) to (108.85, 103.10) removes GND under part of every high-speed finger. Eight local voids under the J1 data lands also interrupt the nearest plane. The fastest traces clear Edge.Cuts by at least 1.21 mm. Sideband `/WAKE#` comes within 0.217 mm of the key at (109.97, 104.72).
- **Signal integrity 8/10.** Data pairs span 34.66–39.15 mm and REFCLK is 40.08 mm. All nine pairs match within 0.001 mm, with symmetric via counts and no fast-net branch stubs. The 0.185/0.145 mm geometry holds along runs such as lane 0 IN on B from (115.88, 104.18) to (129.83, 104.18). Like A and B, the IN pairs and clock make two full face changes. The pad voids can compensate the capacitance of the wide lands. Their dimensions deserve a launch model, particularly the 1.20 mm of J2.43 centreline over the finger void. The long-line impedance estimate is about 92 Ω on the file stack.
- **Power 9/10.** The strongest 12 V distribution of the four. At x = 160, copper spans y = 98.60–109.30 on both F and In2, 10.70 mm on each layer. There are 28 rail vias, a back-side slot pour, and nearby GND on In1. F and In2 also distribute 3.3 V with 13 vias. J3.4–J5 has a 1.0 mm trace. C1.1 joins local 12 V copper with a via at (147.40, 112.40), 1.20 mm away. The GND via at (144.28, 112.45) is 0.97 mm from C1.2. The 0.30 mm M.2 finger escapes are short parallel connections to a pour. The lower-current AUX path through R1 uses 0.40 mm traces.
- **Routing and manufacturability 5/10.** Electrical DRC passes. The two via sizes are 0.50/0.25 and 0.60/0.30 mm, with no via centres in component lands. A roughly 76° GND junction at (144.00, 115.05) is local to a via. **The retention feature is missing.** At (186.20, 98.76) there are mask circles, a straight right Edge.Cuts segment and no 3.50 mm drill hit in either drill file. The resulting board has no screw relief at the expected mounting point. Correct the geometry and check the drill/profile overlay before release. Clean DRC cannot catch this omission.

## D — fourth, 2/10

- **EMI 2/10.** Four GND pours and 155 GND vias still leave ten separate GND groups. Seven isolated M.2 return fingers are J2.9, .21, .27, .33, .39, .45 and .51, all at x = 107.65, from y = 91.27 to 101.77. J1.A15 at (132.21, 118.81) is isolated, and J1.A27/A28 form another group. These are return contacts directly between lane launches. Inner-layer signal and supply routing also slots the planes. A back-side `/Data_0_IN` run has an 8.67 mm interval without In2 GND, centred near (121.98, 107.08). At the `/REFCLK−` via (111.81, 102.21), the nearest GND via is 8.18 mm away. `/Data_3_IN_N` approaches the board outline to 0.332 mm at (110.20, 108.00).
- **Signal integrity 2/10.** The largest planar intra-pair mismatch is just 0.0034 mm, but the conductors follow different transmission lines. `/REFCLK+` is 34.67 mm entirely on B. Its mate is also 34.67 mm in plan, with 1.70 mm on F and two full through-via traversals. Lane 1 IN puts 9.62 mm of P on In1 and 18.38 mm of N on In2. The median nearest same-layer edge gap for lane 0 IN is 0.56 mm and the whole 32.91 mm positive route exceeds 0.25 mm. Asymmetric transitions, unused portions of through barrels at inner-layer taps, and long separated routes dominate the tiny length differences. Plane gaps also leave REFCLK without its adjacent reference for a 4.41 mm back-side interval around (116.60, 102.81).
- **Power 4/10.** The main 12 V feed from J3.1 at (175.59, 96.14) to the C1 region at (146.21, 112.37) consists of 36.1 mm of 0.80 mm B.Cu across three segments. At the file's 18 µm copper this alone is roughly 43 mΩ, or 86 mV at an illustrative 2 A, before connector, branch and return losses. Only one 0.60/0.30 mm 12 V via at (146.51, 115.66) feeds the front slot contacts. The 3.3 V rail uses 0.60 mm runs on three layers and necks to 0.18 mm between J2 fingers near (107.90, 92.52). The 5 V link is 0.50 mm wide. C7.2's nearest GND via is 2.19 mm away at (116.70, 89.17). The broken connector grounds further compromise the card's current return.
- **Routing and manufacturability 2/10.** Nine active missing connections survive refill. DRC identifies the GND zones, and tracing their copper groups identifies the connector pads listed above. The ordinary 0.45/0.25 and 0.60/0.30 mm vias can be fabricated, but their count buys little in the dense launch region. Lane 3 IN N has 90° corners at (110.20, 90.27) and (110.20, 108.00). A back GND junction at (122.97, 115.81) has an approximately 18° included angle. The same retention drill and relief missing on C are missing here. The adapter needs its connector returns restored, its fast pairs rerouted, and its mounting geometry corrected before an order.

## Authorship guess

**B, 93% confidence.** After ranking the boards, I would pick B as the human layout. Its small rounded tuning sections vary by lane while the IN/OUT track lengths converge to two targets separated by about one board traversal. Return vias follow the individual transitions. The local power pours, short redundant edit at (146.01, 110.17), and explicit treatment of the eccentric edge drill are consistent with interactive routing and successive edits. Those traits are circumstantial. A ranks higher because its measured power distribution and nominal pair geometry better support this order.
