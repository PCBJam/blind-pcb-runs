# Raspberry Pi NVMe NAS layout review

**Select D for release work. Hold the fabrication order.** D has the most convincing combination of USB, PCIe, Ethernet and flash routing. A has the best power distribution and is a close second. C needs a stackup decision, a lane correction and zone cleanup. B has an open switch node on the converter that supplies the PCIe switch core.

## Release issues shared by all four boards

- **Input protection has a component-value issue.** J6.1 at (117.65, 64.90) feeds the TVS IC1, C5 and the TPS2420 eFuse U3 on `+12P`. U3 supplies seven buck converters on `+12V`. R1 at (124.00, 75.60) programs IMAX and R2 at (125.00, 75.60) programs IFLT. Both are **52 kΩ**, about **3.85 A nominal** for each threshold. TI requires IMAX to exceed IFAULT across tolerances. Establish the four-drive startup and fault budget, then correct or justify these values before release. U1 is a 3 A-class converter. Its 5 V output serves the CM4, all four fans and the standby regulator U4. SSD and fan currents were unspecified, so the power scores reflect measured copper and available margin. [TPS2420 datasheet, pin descriptions and Table 2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2420.pdf) · [TPS563202 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps563202.pdf)
- **The CM4 variant and connector height affect mechanical and RF sign-off.** Registering the module outline and pin-1 end to the CM4 drawing puts its minimum onboard-antenna clearance rectangle at about **(98.70, 138.95)–(109.70, 145.45)**. Of its 71.5 mm², In1 ground covers **68.3 / 71.1 / 70.4 / 69.3 mm²** on A/B/C/D. All four run signals there. For a wireless CM4 using its PCB antenna this conflicts with the module integration drawing. Specify the module/antenna configuration. The J1 microSD footprint at (113.30, 134.83) also sits beneath the module outline on the component side. Verify the fitted socket height, selected mezzanine height and card access in the enclosure. [CM4 datasheet, §3.1 and Figure 4](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/cm4/cm4-datasheet.pdf)
- **Reconcile the schematic and footprint release data.** The four supplied schematics are identical. KiCad 10.0.8 reports 199 pad-net naming conflicts and 16 footprint/symbol field differences on each board. Some are naming differences, e.g. C6.1 at (126.00, 75.12) is `Net-(C6-Pad1)` on the board and `Net-(U3-CT)` in the schematic. There is also an annotation warning on netlist export. The export lists Module1.84 under both `+1V8` and `+3V3`, whereas the board assigns .84 to `+3V3` at (92.78, 127.05) and .88 to `+1V8` at (92.78, 127.85). Verify the CM4 symbol units and pin mapping before a schematic-to-PCB update. The PCB carries the two rails on distinct nets.
- **Define the USB shell discharge path.** J7 and J15 each use a separate `unconnected-(…-PadS1)` net for their shell tabs, e.g. J7 at (105.98, 57.38) and J15 at (225.07, 131.82). The boards provide signal-ground pins. Shell current needs an intentional enclosure/chassis or PCB return strategy at the entry. A, C and D connect tabs on their respective shell nets. B leaves tab-to-tab gaps as well.

## What the checks measured

I opened copies of all four projects, exported the schematic netlist, ran KiCad DRC with schematic parity on the saved boards, and ran DRC again after refilling separate copies. I compared 254 references, positions, rotations, sides and values, plus 1,302 pad records across the four boards. They agree. Placement is **null** throughout because the job fixed it. The supplied directories contain no designer NOTES.md. The storage path runs from Module1 through ASM1184 U12 at (173.30, 123.45) to J2–J5. J7 carries the CM4 high-speed USB link through choke L7. J15 serves the RP2040 full-speed USB port, whose shorter launch discontinuities carry less weight in the scores.

**Impedance screen.** A uniform cross-section calculation used the file’s outer dielectric height and permittivity, 35 μm outer foil, a solid adjacent plane and 10 μm solder mask with assumed εr = 3.8. I solved the 2-D odd-mode electrostatic cross-section with dielectric and then air to estimate differential impedance. Values are rounded. Etch profile, nearby same-layer pours, launches and the manufacturer’s finished stack can change them. The CM4 guide specifies 90 Ω for USB and PCIe, 100 Ω for Ethernet, and ideal within-pair matching better than 0.15 mm for USB/Ethernet and 0.10 mm for PCIe. The switch-to-M.2 channels also need their interface-specific stack sign-off. [CM4 datasheet, §§2.2–2.4](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/cm4/cm4-datasheet.pdf)

## D — first, 8/10

- **EMI 8/10.** In1 is dedicated to GND, with local openings around holes and vias. In2 is mostly ground with local 5 V, 1.2 V and standby islands. There are 65 GND vias within 2.5 mm of the outer perimeter. At the `PCIe_CLK_P_4` transition (176.63, 132.00), a ground via is 0.83 mm away. Of 105.08 mm of bottom clock trace, 104.16 mm lies over In2 ground. A local weakness is `PCIe_RX_N_4` at (210.75, 91.75), where a single 1.78 mm stretch crosses an In2 ground void. `RPI_ACT_LED` comes within 0.475 mm of the outer edge near (80.95, 130.05). The primary USB route and both Y1 oscillator nets stay over ground. The short J7 contact fanout puts two vias on the A7 net around (110.56, 63.22), introducing a local asymmetric loop.
- **Signal integrity 8/10.** L7.2/.3 to Module1.105/.103 measures **57.085 / 57.085** on F.Cu, with 0.15 mm median gap and zero vias. Most PCIe routes retain that coupling. The fourth-slot clock is 111.39 mm per leg with two vias each. End-to-end slot-2 TX including C54/C55 is 64.28 / 64.11. Ethernet `ETH_P3+/−` measures **42.976 / 42.977** from Module1 to U7 through U8. The U18–U17 flash bus is entirely on top and `/QSPI_SCLK` is 3.975 mm to U17.6 at (215.05, 125.65). The R59/R60 27 Ω resistor-to-RP2040 USB legs are 7.43 / 6.71 and the J15.A6 arm is 5.31 mm versus 1.99 to B6. That contact branch adds a stub for one plug orientation. `SD_CLK` is 53.02 versus 29.80 for `SD_CMD`, whose bottom route leaves In2 GND for 9.55 mm near (99.78, 126.13). Check the intended SD speed.
- **Power 7/10.** The `+12P` input has a top pour and the `+12V` trunk is 2.10 mm wide at (160.00, 82.55). The `+3.3V_M1` feed from L3 to J2 has a 1.30 mm top section at (194.50, 71.20). At U1, C3.1 is 1.60 mm from VIN, C3.2 has a GND via 0.78 mm away, and U1.3 has one 0.38 mm away. U1.2–L1.1 uses a direct 0.30 mm top trace. The limiting long branch is **105.0 mm of 0.60 mm-wide +5V**, from (117.40, 87.90) to (222.40, 87.90), feeding the far-side fans. In 35 μm copper this section alone is about **86 mΩ** at room temperature, or 86 mV at 1 A before vias and return. The CM4 has its own In2 5 V region. Verify the fan startup load separately.
- **Routing and manufacture 8/10.** No extra electrical DRC errors or opens beyond the shared findings. There are 402 vias at 0.60/0.30 mm land/drill and 354 at 0.45/0.20. The ground pour is 0.30 mm back from the left edge at y = 100. A 45° included-angle fold in `IMON` at (95.10, 127.90) is a local cleanup item. Thirteen via centers land inside exposed SMD pads, including nine in U12.65 and one in C83.2 at (202.40, 128.55). Review solder wicking before selecting the four-layer via finish. D’s physical stack and consistent drill families make its fabrication intent the easiest to reproduce.

## A — second, 7/10

- **EMI 8/10.** In1 has no signal tracks. In2 provides ground beneath most bottom pairs, alongside a 5 V region and 2.40 mm 5 V distribution tracks. At (175.97, 126.49), the fourth-slot clock via has a GND via 0.67 mm away. Of 83.18 mm of bottom `PCIe_CLK_P_4`, 82.34 mm lies over GND. A weaker path is `SD_CLK` near (88.91, 127.50). On its 29.05 mm bottom run, 5.64 mm overlies 5 V and 2.21 mm has no In2 pour. Most stitching is local, with zero GND vias within 2.5 mm of the perimeter. `PCIe_TX_P_2` comes within 1.28 mm of the top edge at (166.56, 55.93). Y1 and its 27 pF capacitors stay on top with GND vias 0.54 mm from both load-cap ground pads.
- **Signal integrity 7/10.** The L7–CM4 USB legs are **47.833 / 47.833**, entirely on top over ground. The short J7 side has two vias on the A6 net, one in the connector pad at (110.55, 62.12), and none on A7. U12 to J2 `PCIe_RX_P/N_1` measures 67.07 / 67.07 with two vias per leg. The 15 PCIe pairs have nominal copper sums within 0.03 mm across each pair, including the AC-cap segments. **Overlaps make some of that precision illusory.** `ETH_P3−` folds back at (94.43, 76.07) near U7. The file sums both Ethernet legs to 48.563, but the connected centerline path from Module1.5 to U7.R7 is about 43.65 versus 48.56 on P. An overlap at (157.27, 121.15) similarly adds 0.40 to the nominal J4.49–C57.1 length. At U18, the two resistor-side USB legs are well balanced at 6.450 / 6.454 with two vias each. Flash SCLK is 3.78 mm on top, but `/QSPI_SD3` takes four vias and three layers for a 7.20 mm connection beside U17.
- **Power 9/10.** J6.1 to U3.1–4 has `+12P` copper on both faces. At y = 68.50 the transverse widths are 4.30 mm top and 3.65 mm bottom. At x = 160, the main `+12V` distribution has 6.00 mm top and 7.37 mm bottom copper around y = 80–81. There are 111 vias on 12 V and 100 on 5 V. An In2 5 V backbone is 2.40 mm wide for 58.42 mm from (123.54, 133.10) to (181.92, 135.34). Local output pours serve the M.2 power pins. The U10.2–L3.1 switch path at y = 76.15 is 2.45 mm long, 0.42 mm wide, on top. U10.3 and C18.2 have GND vias 0.39 and 0.74 mm away. Short switch paths and parallel distribution give A the most margin for concurrent loads. U1’s bootstrap branch still adds several mm of switched copper around C9 at (121.60, 86.35).
- **Routing and manufacture 7/10.** Zero opens and zero additional copper-rule errors after refill. Eighteen via centers are in exposed SMD pads, including J7.A6 and four in the U3 thermal pad. Eleven vias have 0.43 mm lands with 0.20 mm drills, within JLC capability but in its published small-land surcharge range. The minimum routed width is 0.127 mm. `+12V` clears the J6 internal cutout by about 0.28 mm near (120.61, 60.13). The 36.5° included-angle reversal on `PICO_Tacho3` at (206.54, 123.45), and especially the overlapping high-speed segments, deserve cleanup. The power advantage is real. D edges ahead because its pin-to-pin Ethernet matching, flash routing and drill choices are better.

## C — third, 5/10

- **EMI 6/10.** There are 802 GND vias, including 37 within 2.5 mm of the perimeter. `PCIe_CLK_P_4` has a return via 0.66 mm from its J5-side transition at (213.33, 60.25). Ground covers most of its 92.10 mm bottom run. Other lanes cross U12’s In2 rail islands. `PCIe_RX_P_4` spends 8.60 mm over `+3.3VA` and another 1.47 mm over empty In2. `ETH_P0+` leaves In1 GND for 7.93 mm in total, including 4.37 mm near (99.70, 77.56) on the U9–U7 approach. Preserve magnetics isolation while providing a reference up to the PHY-side termination. `LED_V+` comes within 0.475 mm of the outer edge at (138.95, 55.15). USB and the 8.24 mm branched XIN net at Y1 stay on F.Cu over GND. Bootstrap branches on B.Cu, described below, spread switching fields to the other face.
- **Signal integrity 5/10.** The main USB pair is tightly routed on F.Cu, **51.129 / 51.085**, with 0.20 mm width and gap and zero vias. The RP2040 resistor-side USB legs are 7.21 / 6.71 on top. All six flash nets stay on F.Cu, with a 3.90 mm clock. The principal risk is the filed 0.480 mm outer dielectric, which gives high differential impedance with the actual USB and PCIe widths. There is also a physical mismatch on **U12.41/.42 to J4.41/.43**. `PCIe_RX_P_3/N_3` sums to **101.18 / 109.98**. Removing a short overlapping centerline still gives about **8.36 mm path mismatch**. The negative-leg accordion is visible at (157.80–162.05, 128.32–129.15). Both legs have two vias. Other pairs are much better matched, e.g. the fourth-slot clock at 106.29 / 106.29. `ETH_P0+/−` from Module1 to U7 is 45.46 / 42.17. Correct the actual electrical lengths and agree the stack before release.
- **Power 7/10.** The `+12P` top pour measures 4.65 mm across at y = 68.50 and the main `+12V` pour is 5.70 mm wide at (160.00, 84.25). `+3.3V_M1` has a 1.40 mm feed at (194.50, 71.20). Seven individual F.Cu switch-node pours carry current between the buck ICs and inductors, a good use of local copper. U1–L1 has about 1.59 mm of switch-node copper across y = 81.50. The same `Net-(C9-Pad2)` also travels **6.42 mm on bottom** through vias at (122.20, 81.75) and (121.60, 87.55) to the bootstrap capacitor. C3.2’s nearest GND via is 1.14 mm away. The long 5 V fan feed is 0.80 mm wide at (160.00, 108.90). It has less parallel copper than A.
- **Routing and manufacture 5/10.** All PCB nets connect. The saved board adds **40 zone-clearance errors and 19 same-net, equal-priority zone intersections**. Refilling removes the 40 clearance errors. All 19 intersections persist, including overlapping `+1V2` zone definitions anchored at (169.55, 104.60). The saved clearances include 0.195 mm against a 0.200 mm rule. These are significant release-data defects, with no DRC evidence of 19 shorts. The project sets copper-edge clearance to zero. Saved In1 GND starts at x = 80.60 at y = 100, but a fresh fill starts at **x = 80.45**, just 0.05 mm inside the routed edge. Correct the rule, consolidate pours and recheck the plotted layers. Of 1,049 vias, 1,035 are 0.45/0.20 and 14 are 0.5032/0.30. Twenty-two centers fall inside exposed SMD pads, including U3.17 at (123.60, 71.85). The 45° included bend on bottom `PCIe_RX_N_1` at (186.04, 112.89) is minor beside the fill and lane issues.

## B — fourth, 1/10

- **EMI 2/10.** In1 has broad GND. In2 consists of eight rail regions with no GND pour, and neither outer face has a GND pour. The 65.37 mm bottom `PCIe_CLK_P_4` run has 29.98 mm over no adjacent copper, including a continuous **23.76 mm gap** centered at (200.95, 106.20). Its via at (192.55, 118.40) is 9.78 mm from the nearest GND via. `USB_DP` changes layers twice and spends 12.09 of its 12.97 bottom mm over 5 V. Ethernet protector returns near U8/U9 at (93.00, 80.34) and (98.70, 81.10) appear in the GND opens. Only two GND vias lie within 2.5 mm of the perimeter. The In1 GND fill itself reaches the outer edge. High-frequency return and ESD current would have poorly controlled paths.
- **Signal integrity 2/10.** L7 to the CM4 measures **50.215 / 49.455** with two vias per leg, mostly 0.40 mm tracks necked to 0.127 mm. The median gap is **1.16 mm**, versus 0.15–0.20 mm on the other boards. `PCIe_TX_P_4` has about 6.83 mm median same-layer separation from its mate. `PCIe_RX_P/N_4` is 85.29 / 95.26 from U12 to J5. At J7, A7 (110.05, 62.12) is open. At J15, A7 (220.32, 127.25) is open and U18.46 at (211.94, 129.10) never reaches R60.2 at (217.40, 127.80). Pico USB therefore loses a complete signal leg. Flash SCLK is a useful 3.77 mm top connection. The sensitive XIN net `Net-(C74-Pad1)` instead takes two vias and 5.35 mm on bottom near (203.12, 125.85).
- **Power 1/10.** The 1.2 V converter cannot deliver its output as drawn. DRC separates U2.2 at (169.06, 96.95), L2.1 at (166.50, 96.95) and bootstrap C10.2 at (173.06, 97.45), all on `Net-(C10-Pad2)`. It also finds an open at U12.64 on `+1V2`, six `+3.3VA` missing connections, and disconnected regulator feedback-divider grounds, including R32.2 at (190.25, 76.65). In2 does give the slot rails and protected 12 V broad distribution. At the input, however, the `+12P` cut across y = 68.50 is just a 0.60 mm top trace near x = 118.00. U1.2 to L1 uses 0.15 mm on an ampere-class switch path. C18.2’s nearest GND via is 1.54 mm away. Wide plane interiors cannot fix the missing switch node or feedback returns.
- **Routing and manufacture 1/10.** **29 opens after refill**, including `CM4_RUN`, fan `PICO_PWM1` from U18.6 to M1.4, and six USB shell-tab links. There is also a dangling `CM4_BOOT` via at (165.00, 130.65). The 528 vias at 0.45/0.20 and 71 at 0.60/0.30 are ordinary through-hole geometries and none of their centers fall in exposed SMD pads. But the board’s copper-edge minimum is zero. In1 GND begins at **x = 80.4005** for the **x = 80.4000** edge at y = 100, and a 0.60 mm bottom `+12V` track clears the internal J6 cutout by only **0.032 mm** near (122.02, 60.07). Both need correction for routed-edge manufacture. Two `PCIe_RST_M2` traces approach J3.50 at (166.00, 66.15) from almost the same direction, forming an 8° included angle at the pad. This is incomplete electrical routing.

## Authorship estimate

**C, 95% confidence.** The individually shaped switch-node pours, small pad-specific fill keepouts around the M.2 power contacts, and curved skew tuning in selected PCIe corridors look like successive local edits by a designer working in the PCB editor. The treatment of Y1 and the via-free flash bus fits that interpretation. The erroneous lane-3 tuning and duplicate zones remain part of C’s score. Authorship is an inference from the geometry, independent of the ranking.
