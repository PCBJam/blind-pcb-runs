# OrangeCrab six-layer layout review

**Hold the order. Choose D as the basis for release.** Its DDR3 routes are matched by group, its two ground layers carry only ground, and its three buck converters have short local current loops. Its saved copper and its assembly clearances still need work. B is electrically connected and has the cleanest project-rule DRC, but it requires HDI fabrication and spends much of its routing margin on the FPGA, memory and flash paths. C has good local component placement and open supply nets. A has both open circuits and parts occupying the same physical space.

Overall scores weigh electrical function, assembly and the work needed to release the actual files. Placement is scored for all four because 144 of the 163 footprints were available to place.

## What was checked

I opened all four boards with KiCad 10.0.8, rendered the component and track layouts, ran project-rule DRC with all severities on isolated copies, and measured the tracks, pad geometry, via layer spans, filled polygons and outline. Coordinates below are absolute KiCad millimetres, Y increasing downwards. The rounded Feather outline spans (134.51, 91.52) to (185.31, 114.38). The four submissions agree on the 163 footprint IDs, 1,097 pad records, pad nets, and locations, orientations and sides of the 19 fixed items. Cross-comparison after undoing footprint rotation and flipping also gave matching pad sizes, positions, shapes, drills and layer sets. D retains only the J3 and J5 footprint lock flags. The starting board, schematics, PDF and designers’ notes mentioned in the brief were absent from the supplied tree. Circuit identification uses the embedded component values and pin-to-net connections. Independent schematic parity and comparison to the original were unavailable.

- All four have 111 library-availability warnings, 51 library-footprint mismatches and 39 undersize-text warnings. One example is the 0.60 mm “RST” label at (141.114, 111.640) against the 0.80 mm project rule. All hide footprint reference designators. J5’s open courtyard at (145.21, 98.88) and the H4/FID6 courtyard overlap at (182.77, 94.06) and (182.81, 96.83) are common fixed geometry. The projects have no recorded DRC exclusions. Missing courtyards are an ignored check, which matters particularly on A.
- J1/J2 have intentional plated holes intersecting the outline. The J1.1 edge hole is 1.00 mm diameter at (151.02, 91.39). This detail accounts for 84 edge reports on each of A, C and D. B has a pad-specific exception for those two footprints. Order castellations explicitly and have CAM confirm the edge drawing. JLCPCB lists 0.09/0.09 mm multilayer line/space, a local BGA fan-out allowance, a 0.20 mm preferred via drill and 0.20 mm inner-layer via-hole-to-copper spacing. The 0.15 mm mechanical drills used here are a selectable fine-drill process. Confirm filled and capped processing wherever vias land in SMD pads. [JLCPCB manufacturing capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)
- USB runs from J3 to the ECP5 through the 20 Ω R21/R29 resistors. R20 is a 1.5 kΩ pull-up on D+. This is consistent with a USB full-speed device. OSC1 is a powered 48 MHz oscillator with a single `REF_CLK` output. The fastest timing review is the x16 DDR3 interface between U3 and U4. The BQ24232 charger can operate at 500 mA input, while the TLV62569 bucks are rated up to 2 A each. Actual rail loads depend on the FPGA configuration and peripherals. [BQ24232 data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24232.pdf) · [TLV62569 data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv62569.pdf)
- Point-to-point lengths follow the connected copper centreline, including short pad-centre approaches where appropriate. Net totals include branches when explicitly identified. Neither includes package delay or via-barrel delay. Ground coverage measures the projection of a track centreline onto saved ground fill on an adjacent copper layer. This identifies slots and missing references. Impedance, system timing margin and emissions require the production stack and operating conditions. Decoupling distances are same-net pad-centre distances in XY. Opposite-side capacitors also need the vertical connections.

## D — first, overall 7/10

**EMI · 8/10.** In1.Cu and In4.Cu each have about 891 mm² of saved ground fill. Their 21.91 and 20.01 mm of tracks respectively are all GND. This leaves both outer signal layers with a consistent reference, and In2.Cu references In1.Cu beside the split supplies on In3.Cu. Of 141 GND vias, 139 centres land in both inner ground fills. Of the 21.879 mm USB positive trunk on In2.Cu, 21.35 mm projects onto In1 ground. The launch at (139.390, 102.665) has its nearest through GND via or grounded PTH centre 3.05 mm away. A closer return would reduce launch loop area. The closest track copper to the perimeter is about 0.91 mm, at the GND and P1.35V runs ending near (184.30, 103). The delivered inner fills approach the edge much more closely, at 0.15 mm.

**Signal integrity · 9/10.** The two connector-side USB trunks each run 21.879 mm on In2.Cu with typical 0.13 mm width and 0.28 mm centre spacing, about 0.15 mm edge gap. One long parallel section is near (150, 94.49). TP26/TP27 at (137.51, 103.58)/(137.51, 100.98) add roughly 2.46/2.40 mm branches. The connector fan-out still has orientation-dependent skew. The J3 A6/A7 paths to the corresponding resistors measure about 25.09/27.50 mm. Beyond the resistors both legs stay on F.Cu, 4.08/4.73 mm to U3.

U3 to U4 shows deliberate DDR grouping. D0–D7 measure 15.321–15.850 mm and travel mainly on B.Cu. D8–D15 measure 15.839–15.914 mm and travel mainly on In2.Cu, including `RAM_D11` at (173.625, 104.000). Each DQ has two through-vias. The LDQS traces are 15.850/15.850 mm on predominantly In2.Cu and UDQS is 15.395/15.350 mm on predominantly B.Cu. The address lines cluster at 14.989–15.556 mm. Whole-net `RAM_CK+`/− lengths of 21.704/21.707 mm conceal a receiver-path difference. U3 to U4 measures 16.12/16.93 mm, with another 5.58/4.78 mm to the R5/R13 termination network. OSC1 at (168.177, 102.775) reaches U3.A9 in 3.108 mm on F.Cu without a via. The flash clock to U6.6 is 15.88 mm with a 2.55 mm R31 branch. The missing dielectric stack specification keeps the USB and DDR impedance targets open for sign-off.

**Power · 8/10.** U5 at (144.285, 104.474) has C77 on VBUS 1.05 mm from its input, C13 on VBATT 1.23 mm from a battery pin and C12 on VSYS 1.10 mm from an output pin. The In3 supply pours include about 23 mm² of PUSB_VBUS and 47 mm² of VSYS. The three regulators have their input capacitors C6/C7/C11 on the same side, 1.13/1.01/1.10 mm from VIN. Each corresponding pair of ground pads is within 1.19 mm. The three switch-node tracks are just 1.30/1.44/1.24 mm long. They are only 0.121 mm wide, but their F.Cu switch-node pours add 1.89/1.93/1.99 mm² respectively. For example, U1.4 at (151.135, 109.855) drives L1.1 at (149.948, 110.130). Power polygons on both sides of the inductors keep current local. Some distribution still necks to 0.089 mm at BGA escapes. The median C48–C69 power-pad distance to the nearest corresponding U3 supply ball is 0.89 mm. Individual loops vary, including 1.70 mm from C50’s supply pad to the nearest same-net via.

**Placement · 8/10.** U3 (161.20, 100.90), U4 (174.85, 102.40) and OSC1 leave room for the memory groups and a short clock. U5 and the bucks occupy the connector end. D1 and D2 sit beside the USB connector, SW1 is accessible from the top at (181.90, 106.45), and the back-side microSD body area is clear of mounted components. Density costs assembly margin. There are 68 courtyard overlaps, including C13/J3 and U5/C13. For one measurable example, the drawn F.Fab extents of U4 and C74 at (169.852, 102.05) still have about 0.25 mm between them despite the courtyard error. Check the actual component envelopes, placement tolerance and rework access for the remaining pairs.

**Routing and manufacture · 5/10.** All 441 vias are mechanical through-vias, 273 at 0.30/0.15 mm land/drill and 168 at 0.28/0.15 mm. The 0.089 mm necks are mostly short compared with the predominant 0.121 mm tracks. A 45.6° inside corner on P1.1V at (161.20, 100.90), In3.Cu, merits cleanup. The 13 hole-to-hole warnings range from 0.2023 to 0.2488 mm against the project’s 0.25 mm. A ground hole at (164.871, 98.775) is about 0.154 mm from different-net ECP5_VREF track copper on In3.Cu, below JLCPCB’s listed 0.20 mm. There are no reported silk-to-pad clips.

**D has a reproducible release-file problem.** The saved board has 56 clearance errors, 13 hole-clearance errors, six mask-bridge errors and two coincident priority-1 B.Cu GND zones. For example, inner ground is only 0.10 mm from H4’s NPTH. A direct KiCad refill stalled for over seven minutes. On an isolated copy I replaced only the 21 all-zero zone identifiers with unique IDs and refilled. Connectivity remained at zero opens. The hole-clearance errors, mask bridges and three non-header edge errors disappeared, and 56 clearance errors fell to one. The remaining clearance is 0.0882 mm between B.Cu GND at (159.555, 107.166) and U9.19 P1.35V. The coincident zones, 68 courtyard overlaps, 84 header-edge reports and dangling items persisted. Regenerate and check the production copper after resolving these items and restoring the fixed-item locks.

## B — second, overall 6/10

**EMI · 5/10.** In1.Cu and In4.Cu contain 798 and 837 mm² of GND, and 109 through GND vias reach both. There are 27 further GND microvias. The two reference layers also carry 191 and 106 mm of tracks respectively, including supplies and signals. Only about 58% of the F.Cu USB, RAM, flash, SD-clock and reference-clock track length projects onto In1 ground. A 2.575 mm F.Cu `RAM_D8` run from (166.975, 106.175) to (164.400, 106.175) is entirely over a ground opening. Only 1.54 mm of the 4.72 mm back-side `REF_CLK` run has In4 ground directly opposite. The main USB trunk fares better, with 17.53 of 18.06 mm over In4 ground. Its J3 positive launch at (139.755, 102.700) is 2.57 mm from the nearest through ground connection measured centre to centre. The closest routed copper to the outline is the positive USB test branch at (135.60, 108.72), with 1.04 mm clearance.

**Signal integrity · 6/10.** J3.A6/A7 to R21/R29 are both 19.317 mm. The paired section on In3.Cu measures 18.058 mm per leg, typically 0.14 mm wide with 0.30 mm centres. B specifies an adjacent 0.12 mm dielectric and a 0.30 mm clearance to coplanar zones. The opposite connector contacts give 20.53/21.59 mm paths. TP26 and TP27 branch 7.92/9.78 mm from the A-contact paths. After the resistors, the positive path is 4.45 mm, mostly front, and the negative is 5.24 mm, mostly back. The favourable matching on the inner section ends at the breakouts.

The LDQS pair is 11.538/11.538 mm and UDQS is 9.522/9.475 mm. Individual data routing is less controlled. `RAM_D11` is 19.181 mm with six vias, including a buried transition at (163.225, 108.925), while `RAM_D8` is 8.863 mm. Address lengths range from 4.80 to 13.27 mm. The CK receiver paths are fairly well matched at 12.98/12.86 mm, but extend 6.82/6.64 mm to R5/R13. OSC1 reaches U3 in 4.88 mm with one via. The flash clock is a larger concern. U3.U16 (157.65, 104.40) to U6.6 (181.115, 102.488) takes 51.40 mm across multiple layers, plus 2.34 mm to R31. The clock net contains six vias.

**Power · 5/10.** U1 and U2 each reach the inductor in 1.93 mm of 0.35 mm back copper. U7 to L3 takes 3.81 mm. C6 is 1.27 mm from U1 VIN, and its ground pad has a GND via within 0.38 mm. C50 at (160.40, 103.90) puts its supply pad within 0.07 mm in XY of U3.M15 and 0.13 mm of a P1.1V via. The input distribution deserves equal attention. All 35.14 mm of PUSB_VBUS track is 0.105 mm wide, with seven vias and no VBUS pour. The J3.A9-to-U5.13 path alone has about 5.30 mm of 0.105 mm track on 18 µm inner copper, roughly 48 mΩ for that portion before accounting for parallel paths. VSYS has a useful In2 pour, but 54.18 of its 55.40 mm of tracks are just 0.105 mm wide. Verify the loaded drop from J3 and the battery to the bucks, especially where current converges.

**Placement · 7/10.** U3 (161.40, 101.15) and U4 (171.95, 102.60) are close. U5 at (144.70, 105.10) is near the USB and battery connectors with C77 and C12 within 1.30 mm of their respective power pins. The SD shell area is clear and SW1 is on top at (178.80, 105.75). Several decouplers are well placed underneath U3, but the C48–C69 median is 2.23 mm to the nearest matching supply ball and C69 reaches 5.46 mm. Putting U6 at (181.75, 105.35) behind the far end of the memory field contributes to the long flash routing. The remote USB test pads also consume length and edge space.

**Routing and manufacture · 5/10.** Both saved and refilled DRC give zero opens and only the two common fixed-footprint errors. There are no silk clips or dangling tracks. The board gets there with 468 through-vias, 127 F.Cu–In1.Cu and 70 In4.Cu–B.Cu blind microvias, and 30 In1.Cu–In4.Cu buried vias. The blind land/drill is 0.23/0.10 mm, the buried and through land/drill is 0.28/0.15 mm. The blind/buried construction needs a quoted HDI process. The microvia at (169.35, 109.25) on RAM_VDDQ is one example. Some via centres are in SMD lands, including FPGA and DRAM balls. The specified 1.6 mm stack has outer dielectrics of 0.085 mm. Confirm drill spans, copper filling, stack and impedances together. Many tracks are 0.090–0.108 mm, including long supply runs. There is also a 45° inside corner clear of lands on `RAM_A15` at (165.000, 101.225), In3.Cu. These process and layout costs are substantial despite the clean electrical DRC.

## C — third, overall 3/10

**EMI · 4/10.** In1.Cu has 869 mm² of GND and only ground tracks. In3.Cu adds 833 mm² of GND, interrupted by routing. All 134 GND via centres land in both fills. About 91% of the selected fast-signal length on F.Cu and 95% on In2.Cu has adjacent ground fill. The back side sees a split power layer on In4.Cu. For example, the 8.05 mm of B.Cu `REF_CLK`, including the long run at x = 164.88 from y = 96.52 to 102.32, projects onto 3.76 mm of P3.3V, 3.14 mm of P1.35V and 0.30 mm of RAM_VDD. Return current must change reference at those boundaries. B.Cu `RAM_D11` similarly crosses four different supplies. On top of that, DRC finds 14 missing GND connections, including U5’s exposed pad at (146.00, 103.80) and J3/J4 shell connections. VSYS approaches to 0.372 mm of the right edge near (184.738, 103.55).

**Signal integrity · 5/10.** J3.A6/A7 to R21/R29 measure 17.60/16.65 mm. The opposite B6/B7 contacts give 18.60/15.65 mm. TP26/TP27 at (151.10, 97.05)/(151.10, 95.10) sit on the respective signal paths, avoiding B’s long test branches. Coupling is loose in places. The positive A-contact path has 4.87 mm on In2.Cu with a median 0.76 mm centre distance to the nearest negative path on that layer. Beyond R21/R29 the paths to U3 measure 7.02/9.76 mm, with two vias per net. There is no specified production impedance stack.

DDR data lengths span 7.04–17.18 mm. Even within D0–D7, `RAM_D5` is 7.04 mm and `RAM_D0` is 14.43 mm. LDQS is 14.22/15.49 mm and UDQS is 11.59/10.57 mm. CK reaches U4 in 16.62/17.81 mm, although the short 0.55 mm termination branches are good. OSC1 at (165.50, 95.50) and U3.A9 are both on top, yet `REF_CLK` changes sides twice over its 10.24 mm length. Flash placement works better. U3 to U6.6 is 9.21 mm on the clock path and the R31 branch is only about 0.35 mm.

**Power · 3/10.** The three switch paths are 1.445 mm each, 0.40 mm wide and via-free on B.Cu. At U2, for example, the switch pin at (184.10, 104.70) feeds L2.1 at (183.90, 106.063). Each 10 µF input capacitor is 1.12 mm from VIN and its ground pad is 1.12 mm from IC ground. All PUSB_VBUS track is 0.40 mm wide, as is 74.48 mm of the 75.38 mm VSYS routing. There are large power pours. Some P1.1V and P3.3V branches still drop to 0.0889 mm. More importantly, the unconnected items include six on P2.5V, three on P1.35V, two on P1.1V, one on ECP5_VREF and one on VSYS, in addition to GND. The VREF break near (167.35, 101.35) compromises the memory interface even where the digital tracks reach both balls.

**Placement · 7/10.** C48–C69 have a median XY distance of 0.46 mm from their supply pads to a matching U3 ball. C30–C37 have a median of 0.00 mm at U4, with the capacitors on the opposite side. For example, C50’s supply pad at (158.85, 102.30) is 0.25 mm from U3.P9 in projection. The nearest supply and ground vias to its two pads are 0.56 and 0.51 mm away. The microSD body area is clear and U5 sits by the input connectors. U2 at (183.60, 103.95) and L2 at (183.90, 107.00) are compact locally but put one regulator across the board from U5. U6 at (158.50, 109.70) is close to the FPGA, with C74 on P3.3V only 0.92 mm from its supply pin. The charge indicator D2 at (150.45, 104.20) is about 11.6 mm from the front “CHG” legend, a usability issue even after routing is completed.

**Routing and manufacture · 3/10.** Refill reproduces all 27 opens, ten dangling tracks, eight dangling vias and a copper short between a GND via at (180.98, 102.50) and the no-net top copper logo. J2.4 at (148.48, 113.11) has only one thermal spoke on both In1.Cu and In3.Cu. There are 55 silk-over-mask clips and eight silk overlaps. “RST” at (141.114, 111.640) clips R39.1. The 457 vias are all through, and 440 use a 0.15 mm drill. Four via centres land in SMD pads, including C35.2 at (170.15, 105.75). The 0.0889 mm tracks need fine-line CAM review, particularly outside the BGA field. Most corners are ordinary bends. One 83.7° inside angle clear of lands occurs on `ADC_MUX3` at (162.298, 96.690), F.Cu. The missing supply and ground links take precedence over corner cleanup.

## A — fourth, overall 1/10

**EMI · 2/10.** The untracked In1.Cu ground pour covers 856 mm² and both faces have ground fill. There are only 26 GND vias and 18 unconnected GND items. Inner power fills occupy parts of In2.Cu, In3.Cu and In4.Cu. For example, `RAM_A3` runs 9.35 mm on In3.Cu from (156.85, 106.00) to (166.20, 106.00) with no adjacent ground fill. `RAM_D9` on B.Cu similarly lacks adjacent GND along its 8.55 mm horizontal run at y = 97.10. The nearest GND via to C50.2 at (167.92, 109.30) is 12.20 mm away. A positive detail is a 1.02 mm minimum track-to-outline margin, at VBATT by J1.1. USB, the oscillator and the input power path are incomplete, so the corresponding working loops cannot be verified.

**Signal integrity · 1/10.** Both `/IO/_USB_D_P` and `/IO/_USB_D_N` have zero track and zero vias. Even J3.A6 (140.555, 102.70) and B6 (140.555, 103.70) are unjoined. On the FPGA side `USB_D+` and `USB_D-` have only 1.115/1.719 mm of track. There is no end-to-end USB path to match. `REF_CLK` has a 1.115 mm escape from U3.A9 at (167.25, 102.75), open to OSC1.3 at (167.00, 107.00). `SPI_CONFIG_SCK` is also open. Both DDR clock polarities and seven DQ nets are among the open nets. Existing LDQS legs measure 11.22/13.33 mm and take different layers, including a 6.92 mm back-side run on the positive leg. These fragments cannot support a DDR timing release.

**Power · 2/10.** There is useful local work. U1/U2/U7 feed L1/L2/L3 in about 2.42/2.42/2.47 mm of 0.30 mm front copper, and VBATT has 0.50 mm routing. For example, U1.4 at (145.25, 110.90) reaches L1.1 at (147.463, 110.40). But PUSB_VBUS and VSYS have no track or same-net zone. DRC reports four and 13 missing connections respectively, as well as 30 on P3.3V and 17 on P1.1V. U5’s VSYS output is 12.04 mm from C12’s matching pad and the connection is unfinished. C6’s ground pad at (140.28, 110.65) is 3.48 mm from U1 ground and 10.80 mm from the nearest GND via. The input and downstream rails cannot power the board as delivered.

**Placement · 1/10.** A places back-side U5 at (147.67, 99.88), C77 at (148.92, 103.08) and multiple passives inside the back-side J4 microSD shell area. The socket silkscreen extends to x = 152.57 between y = 96.88 and 108.88. C29 at (140.37, 103.81) is also in the drawn card area. J4 has no courtyard to catch these conflicts. On the front, R31 (174.248, 100.055) and R4 (175.750, 97.820) are inside the 10 × 10 mm U3 package outline centred at (171.50, 102.50). Some components cannot be assembled together. The U3/U4 centres are a promising 10.00 mm apart, but the median C48–C69 supply-pad distance to U3 is 6.22 mm. C37 is 12.31 mm from its closest matching U4 ball.

**Routing and manufacture · 1/10.** Refill reproduces 210 opens, 65 dangling tracks, nine dangling vias, 71 silk-over-mask clips and eight silk overlaps. The front “CHG” text at (140.035, 109.405) clips R14.2. Of 222 through-vias, 177 have a 0.28/0.15 mm land/drill. There are 36 via centres in U3 SMD lands alone, so the assembly process would need plated-over holes even after the placement is corrected. There are 313 segments at 0.089 mm. The sharp 36.9° junction on `RAM_D13` at (168.25, 98.25) lies at a land. A scan of two-segment bends found no acute apex outside pads and vias. Open nets and impossible placements dominate the rejection.

## Release decision and authorship

D ranks ahead of B because its reference layers, per-group DDR routing, regulator copper and through-via construction give the better basis for a standard six-layer product. Release D only after regenerating the fills, resolving the remaining copper violation, checking the dense placements against actual bodies and obtaining CAM approval for the fine drills, clearances, castellations and impedance stack. B ranks ahead of C because its electrical connectivity survives refill. C ranks ahead of A because its missing connections are confined to six nets, its signal paths largely exist, and the main packages can occupy their assigned locations.

**Human-layout guess: D, 98% confidence.** The strongest evidence is the coordination across circuits. The two DDR data groups use distinct routing layers and delay targets that track their strobes. The switch pins and inductors have small shaped copper areas, and the charger, clock and front-panel indicators all make sense in their physical surroundings. That combination is more distinctive than a low DRC count or tidy-looking tracks. This is an inference from the layouts, made after scoring.
