# Arduino Mega 2560 layout review

**A is the board I would take to release.** It has complete connectivity, compact oscillator and MCU bypass circuits, and enough copper around the 5 V regulator to make it a credible starting point for barrel-powered operation. Its main weakness is the return path for signals going to the headers. B has the best ground distribution, but its regulator heat spreader and several assembly lands need work. D needs a zone refill and assembly corrections. C has opens in the power, reset and main clock circuits. I would hold the order pending the release items below.

Overall scores reflect the consequences for a populated product. An open main oscillator is a release blocker regardless of how readily the copper can be etched. Placement is scorable on all four boards because the designers chose the positions of both MCUs, both regulators and their supporting parts.

## What was checked

I opened all four boards in KiCad 10.0.8 via pcbnew, inspected copper renders, and ran the CLI DRC with each project's own rules and all severities. I repeated DRC after a zone refill on private copies. Coordinates below are KiCad absolute **(x, y) in mm**, viewed from above. Each board has the same 101.60 × 53.34 mm outline, six mounting cutouts, and 66 footprints with 410 pad records. The footprint identifiers, pad numbers, net assignments, pad sizes and drills agree across the four. The 17 fixed parts agree in position, angle and face. D has cleared their lock flags. There was no separate unrouted source board in the review directory for an independent footprint-library baseline.

¹ KiCad reports missing connections separately. Every board has four fixed-footprint courtyard conflicts. J2 at (118.55, 84.16) overlaps J3 and J6, J1 overlaps FID3 at (99.50, 93.56), and J4 overlaps FID2 at (100.39, 129.25). These warrant a body, mating-plug and fiducial-visibility check against the assembly drawings. DRC courtyard overlap alone does not establish body interference. All four also have 51 library-footprint comparison warnings and five silk-to-edge warnings. No project has DRC exclusions.

² I intersected track centrelines with the actual opposite-layer filled GND polygons, excluding power, ground and shield nets from the signal-length denominator. Fill area uses the board area inside Edge.Cuts, less the cutouts. The measurements describe the saved copper. D drops to 39% / 53% GND fill and 50% directly referenced signal length after refill. The metric misses coplanar return copper, power copper coupled through bypass capacitors and return detours around slots. C's denominator includes only routed track.

**Shared circuit question before order.** Both lands of the open solder jumper JP1 are assigned GND, and both lands of JP2 are assigned RESET in every file. On A these jumpers are at (112.70, 92.10) and (122.20, 115.25). On a completely routed board, soldering either jumper makes no change to connectivity. If either is intended to configure the product, the board netlist needs a separate design decision. This is common to all four scores.

**USB path measurements.** J1 carries USB full speed to the ATmega16U2, U4, through 22 Ω array RN2. Each cell gives the approximate D+ / D− conductor length and absolute mismatch. I traced J1.A6/A7 and B6/B7 separately through the corresponding RN2 pads to U4.29/30. Lengths include short in-pad approaches and exclude the resistor bodies, via barrels and side branches. This matters because the connector has duplicate contacts and RV1/RV2 add protection branches. U1 and U6 are linear regulators. The relevant current loops are the jack and diode feed, the fused USB supply through Q1, and transient MCU and header loads. The other fast interface is the header SPI bus, including PB1/SCK at U3.20, J12.3 and J5.33. The board has no external flash or memory IC.

At 12 Mbit/s these millimetre-scale mismatches carry less weight than prolonged separation, a missing reference or an unnecessary layer transition. The files specify 1.6 mm board thickness. A 90 Ω differential impedance and electrical compliance would require the actual dielectric stack, trace geometry and validation.

**Fabrication basis.** I assumed standard routed FR-4 with 1 oz outer copper. JLCPCB publishes 0.10/0.10 mm minimum track/space and 0.20 mm copper clearance from routed edges for this comparison. All four use conventional through vias with drills of at least 0.30 mm. Solderability, thermal performance and EMI still depend on the assembly process and operating conditions. ## A — first, 7/10

- **EMI · 4/10.** The local barrel-power path is good. J4.1 to D4.2 is 8.2 mm, then D4.1 to U1.3 is only 4.1 mm. C6, C7, C8 and C11 have GND vias within 0.75–0.80 mm of their return pads. Across the board, 2.83 m of bottom tracks carve up the ground fill. The top has about 2,061 mm² of +5V pour and 903 mm² of GND. Only 17% of the PB1 clock tree has opposite GND fill, including a 17.1 mm gap centred at (172.99, 112.92). Its signal via at (157.35, 100.97) is 7.9 mm from the nearest GND via or through-pad. SCL also runs 62.6 mm along y = 79.29, only 0.86 mm from the outer edge. The local stitching helps the MCUs more than the expansion headers.
- **Signal integrity · 7/10.** The U3-to-Y1 oscillator legs are just 3.4 and 2.9 mm, both on top. Fixed Y2 reaches U4.1/2 in 6.9 and 9.3 mm, also without vias. Both RN2-to-U4 USB legs have continuous opposite GND fill. On the connector side, D+ and D− run alongside at x = 109.00 and 108.56 for part of the approach, then separate to enter RN2. The B7 branch alone drops through vias at (106.30, 92.55) and (106.32, 94.07). RV2 adds about a 2 mm D+ branch. The SPI clock takes 33.7 mm to J12.3 and about 73 mm to J5.33. These header routes and their return paths limit the score.
- **Power · 8/10.** The jack-to-diode trace is 1.0 mm wide, and the regulator input also uses 1.0 mm copper from D4. The narrow 0.17–0.26 mm +5V sections coexist with broad distribution copper. U1's output tab at (125.65, 122.25) has eight +5V vias within 5 mm. Within that radius I measured about 76 mm² of top and 48 mm² of back +5V copper, including pads. C4.1 is 2.54 mm from U1.3, C2.1 is 6.01 mm from the tab, and U6.5 is 2.06 mm from its 1 µF output capacitor C15. The four U3 digital supply pins each have a 100 nF capacitor 2.59–3.44 mm away. U4 is weaker, with the closest +5V bypass 7.11 mm from U4.32. The F1-to-Q1 switched USB supply path is compact at about 5.1 mm.
- **Placement · 8/10.** U4 at (115.40, 97.80) sits between J1 and the fixed Y2. Y1 at (138.60, 113.40) faces U3's oscillator pins. FB2 and C18 serve the analog side locally, with only 2.44 mm between C18.1 and U3.100. S1 at (103.75, 83.00) lines up with the reset legend and is accessible at the upper-left corner. The only back part, JP1 at (112.70, 92.10), is a bare solder jumper and needs no second-side component population. U7.8 has a 9.32 mm reach to the nearest +5V ceramic.
- **Routing and manufacture · 8/10.** Complete with the four fixed courtyard errors. Actual tracks reach 0.15 mm minimum. Via diameters are 0.56–0.70 mm, all with 0.30 mm drills. There are no via centres inside top SMD lands. There is scope to simplify 2,091 track segments and 287 vias. For example, +5V doubles back collinearly at (158.05, 93.90), and the GND routing at C7.2 (139.00, 110.53) has a roughly 34° interior junction. The silk report has 35 overlaps and seven mask clips, including the back rectangle at (112.16, 85.50) clipped by J2.6. I would inspect the fixed mechanical conflicts and plotted legends, then shorten the perimeter SCL run and give the PB1 transition a nearby return before release.

## B — second, 6/10

- **EMI · 7/10.** GND fills on both sides and 132 GND vias give B the best return distribution. The stitching includes a 4 mm grid, for example at (99.70, 116.33), (103.70, 116.33) and (107.70, 116.33). About 92% of the connector-side D+ and D− tracks have GND fill underneath. The jack, D4 and input bulk capacitor also make a compact group. Two significant gaps remain. The main resonator's XTAL2 has no GND fill directly below its track, and B.Cu AREF runs 52.9 mm from (174.85, 79.05) to (121.95, 79.05) without opposite GND fill. AREF is only 0.62 mm from the outer edge there. This is a poor place to carry the ADC reference past header traffic.
- **Signal integrity · 6/10.** Both USB orientations stay on top. D+ takes a detour via (111.00, 98.95) before returning to RN2.5 at (110.30, 95.73). Its vertical approach at x = 108.00 is about 0.83 mm centre-to-centre from D− where they run parallel, and other sections diverge entirely. RV2 adds a roughly 1.65 mm spur. The B-contact mismatch is the largest of the four. Y2-to-U4 is appreciably better at 6.47 / 6.52 mm on top. U3-to-Y1 is asymmetric at 10.35 / 4.10 mm. PB1 reaches J12.3 in roughly 16 mm with one via, a useful improvement at the programming header.
- **Power · 6/10.** The jack feeds D4 through 1.5 mm track and the D4-to-U1 input path uses 1.2 mm track. C1.1 is 5.51 mm from U1.3. All four U3 digital supplies have a 100 nF capacitor within 2.10–3.59 mm, and U4.4 to C11.1 is just 1.70 mm. The barrel-powered thermal case is the concern. U1's tab at (134.05, 120.90) has about 21 mm² of +5V copper on top and 2.6 mm² on the back within 5 mm, with one +5V via there. A wide bus elsewhere does little for this tab. At 9 V **at the regulator input** and 300 mA, U1 would dissipate about 1.2 W. Also, the 0.90 mm +5V bus runs 54.47 mm along y = 127.50 and another 31.90 mm vertically at x = 197.40. It uses substantial width but considerable distribution length.
- **Placement · 8/10.** U3 at (179.00, 96.00) faces the large J5 header. C18.1 and C5.1 are 2.09 and 2.55 mm from AVCC and AREF respectively. U4, RN2 and the crystal are grouped near J1. U1 at (130.90, 120.90) sits between its bulk capacitors. All footprints are on top, and reset S1 at (103.60, 83.20) is accessible. U7 at (124.50, 87.00) is 14.15 mm from its nearest +5V ceramic. Moving U3 toward J5 also puts the remote analog header on the far side of the MCU's analog pins.
- **Routing and manufacture · 6/10.** Complete with the four fixed courtyard errors. Actual tracks are at least 0.20 mm and the via sizes are ordinary 0.60/0.30 and 0.80/0.40 mm. A 45° interior +5V corner at (123.00, 103.30) is minor. The assembly issue is that ten drill centres fall inside top SMD lands. These include both 0603 C8 lands at (168.10, 96.00) and (169.30, 96.00), four in U4's exposed pad, one in the J1 ground contact, and both C2 lands. The project specifies tenting and no capping. Solder wicking and paste loss on a standard build deserve attention, especially at C8 and J1. There are 71 silk overlaps and 69 mask clips. For example, C4 at (128.30, 125.90) clips the IOREF header legend. I would move the small-land vias, enlarge U1's tab copper and correct the header silk before ordering B.

## D — third, 5/10

- **EMI · 6/10.** The fills and 53 GND vias provide useful return copper. Nearly half of signal length still has opposite-layer GND after refill. F1-to-Q1 is only 4.1 mm on top, and the XVCC pour keeps the USB supply local. The long header buses cut slots through that return copper. PB1 has a 23.3 mm stretch without opposite GND centred at (159.62, 120.36), and its via at (171.64, 120.74) is 7.74 mm from a GND via or through-pad. RESET runs almost 30 mm parallel to the left edge at x = 98.39, only 0.54 mm inboard. This exposes the reset network to disturbances near the USB and barrel connectors.
- **Signal integrity · 6/10.** The USB paths are short with small planar mismatch. Both RD tracks are 0.254 mm on top over GND. D− alone uses a 2.14 mm bottom section between (106.43, 93.91) and (107.98, 95.32), while D+ stays on top. The data tracks widen to 0.40 mm on portions of the protection network, and RV1/RV2 have about 2 mm of branch routing each. The U4 crystal is a strength, with 8.16 / 8.15 mm top-side routes to Y2. U3's Y1 at (136.08, 117.06) needs 15.72 / 13.87 mm despite being movable. The PB1, PB2 and PB3 SPI trees total 91.6, 104.8 and 114.6 mm respectively, with three vias on each tree. PB1 alone takes 70.9 mm to reach J12.3.
- **Power · 6/10.** D has the strongest regulator heat spreading. The U1 tab at (103.21, 113.88) has about 76 / 75 mm² of +5V copper on the two faces within 5 mm and eight +5V vias. The board carries 169 mm of 1.27 mm +5V track and uses 0.8–1.0 mm on the D4-to-U1 input path. That path is 25.5 mm long. The weakness is the dynamic load at U3. Pins 31 and 61 are 11.69 and 12.03 mm from their nearest 100 nF supply lands. Even the apparently close U3.10/C8 pair needs copper outside a 12 mm radius of the pin to complete both sides of its bypass loop. C15.2 is a more appropriate 2.44 mm from U6.5.
- **Placement · 5/10.** The central MCU gives the header banks an orderly fan-out, and the four LEDs sit near their user legends. But U3 and FID4 coincide at **(148.53, 103.31)**. The package obscures the mark after placement. U3/C18, J2/D3 and five Y2/passive courtyard pairs account for the other seven added overlaps. C16, C17 and R2 form a tidy row at y = 102.32 that intrudes into Y2's assembly courtyard. C5.1 at (124.89, 87.21) is 20.46 mm from U3.98, a poor location for the AREF bypass. JP1 is the only back-side footprint.
- **Routing and manufacture · 4/10.** All nets close, with the fewest vias. Most vias are generous 0.914/0.406 or 1.118/0.610 mm, and actual track width bottoms out at 0.15 mm despite the project's zero global track minimum. There is a 45° interior VCC corner at (121.73, 124.29). **The saved fill is unsafe to use for plotting.** All 499 clearance errors involve zones. XVCC comes within 0.0255 mm of a J1 NPTH at (104.14, 90.67), and four mask-bridge errors are present. Refilling resolves those classes. It leaves 12 courtyard errors, three one-spoke GND thermals at J1.A1/B12 and U3.99, and RESET at 0.4098 mm from the mounting cutout centred at (163.77, 124.04), short of the project's 0.50 mm rule. A 0.0003 mm dangling SCL segment, 37 silk overlaps and eight silk mask clips remain. The hole clearance, thermals and inaccessible fiducial matter more to release than the raw saved error count.

## C — fourth, 1/10

- **EMI · 2/10.** The bottom ground pour covers 75% of the board, but there is no top GND pour. The 31 GND vias leave long stretches of bottom-side signal with no nearby reference on top. The RD+ transition at (112.15, 97.07) is 5.74 mm from the nearest GND via or through-pad. The F1-to-Q1 USB supply path is 31.29 mm long, compared with 4–11 mm on the other layouts. Clock stubs are present at U3, and seven GND connections are missing. For example, DRC separates C10.2 at (122.28, 99.00) from the GND via at (122.08, 101.60). The nearest routed signal copper to the outer perimeter is a comfortable 2.42 mm. Internal return continuity is the serious issue.
- **Signal integrity · 1/10.** The main oscillator cannot operate as laid out. Y1.3 on XTAL1 at (165.00, 116.80) is open to the U3-side track at (174.25, 113.41), and XTAL2 is also open. All 16 ADC header nets have missing connections, as does PB3 on the programming bus. USB illustrates the danger of judging from length matching alone. RD+ runs 9.89 mm on B.Cu at y = 96.47 while RD− follows y = 94.98 for 7.65 mm. These 0.20 mm tracks are 1.49 mm apart centre-to-centre there. Just 10% / 13% of the RD+ / RD− track length has opposite GND fill. Each leg traverses two vias and the extra RD− via at (122.08, 97.03) is dangling. The U4 crystal does connect, with 16.8 / 13.7 mm pin-to-crystal paths.
- **Power · 2/10.** The board uses 0.50 mm for the diode feed, VCC, most +5V distribution and the main USB feed. U1 at (131.00, 117.00) has a broad top +5V area and C4.1 is 2.0 mm from its input. Nevertheless, there are seven missing +5V links, including U3.31 at (173.50, 111.66) to C6.1 at (171.00, 115.73). U3.61 and C8.1 are also disconnected. U4.4's nearest 100 nF +5V land is C13.2 at (125.85, 117.00), 18.07 mm away. The U6 output network is better, with C15.2 3.37 mm from the output. The local regulator layout cannot compensate for broken supply distribution.
- **Placement · 4/10.** All components fit on top with only the four fixed courtyard conflicts. RN2 at (113.50, 96.00) sits by the USB port, but its rotation faces the RD pads away from U4 at (126.50, 96.50). Y1 at (165.00, 118.00) is well outside U3's oscillator pins. The closest bypass to each U3 digital supply is 4.77–7.74 mm away. S1 at (119.50, 122.50) is beside the power section, about 39 mm from the RESET switch legend at (101.09, 87.97). D6 at (131.50, 106.50) is similarly remote from ON at (172.55, 97.25).
- **Routing and manufacture · 1/10.** Refill leaves 58 missing connections and 50 dangling-via warnings. Ten missing links belong to RESET, seven each to GND and +5V, and two each to XTAL1 and XTAL2. All 214 vias are standard 0.60/0.30 mm. Only about 1.9 mm of +5V track necks to 0.127 mm. There is a nearly folded-back TXD1 junction at (181.00, 111.66). Of the 92 silk mask clips and 67 overlaps, one example is the TX3 legend at (166.88, 88.55) clipped by FB2.1. The dimensions are manufacturable. The fabricated board would faithfully reproduce the opens.

## Release decision and authorship

**A over B** comes down to the barrel-power thermal path, short main oscillator, cleaner assembly lands and more usable silk. B wins on ground continuity and has particularly good MCU bypass placement. **B over D** rests on complete, stable copper after refill, local power loops and fewer assembly conflicts. **D over C** is a functional distinction. D's problems are specific and correctable. C needs the power, ground, clock and I/O routing finished before a meaningful bring-up.

For a check on the bypass assessment, I clipped both copper layers around each U3 supply pin and tested connectivity through the actual pads, tracks, pours and plated transitions. Each test required both the supply pin to reach the nearest 100 nF capacitor and the adjacent GND pin to reach the capacitor's other land. In pin order 10, 31, 61, 80, A closed all four loops inside radii of **4, 4, 3, 3 mm**, and B inside **4, 3, 3, 3 mm**. D needed **15, 20, 15, 8 mm** on the tested radii. C failed the first three even across the whole board. These radii bound the copper paths geometrically. Loop inductance and current density would require electrical modelling.

**I think D is the human layout, with 94% confidence.** Its central U3 fans whole banks of pins into parallel routes for specific header rows. Around the barrel regulator the wide +5V copper and large vias follow the available space on each face. The four indicators align with the board legends, and the repeated via dimensions and trunk widths fit that routing pattern. Together these look like a designer planning placement, front-panel markings and fan-out in the same pass. Authorship is an inference from the layout. D's stale fill, long oscillator loop and misplaced fiducial stand in the scores above.
