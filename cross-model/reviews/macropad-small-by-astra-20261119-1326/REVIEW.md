# Macropad layout review

**Select A for the prototype order. Hold product release for a BOOTSEL circuit correction common to all three designs.** A has the shortest USB path, a well-grouped fused supply, and no via drill breaking into an exposed SMD land. B earns its smaller outline and has the best-referenced flash clock, but its flash-select branch and unfilled vias in solder lands need rework. C uses the most board area and still routes sensitive signals and power across gaps in their ground reference.

Overall scores reflect the risk of ordering and assembling each layout. Placement is scored because the designers controlled 42 of the 54 footprints.

## Findings that apply to the order

- **BOOTSEL can short an active output to ground.** On every schematic, U103.56 and U104.1 connect directly to SW101.1 on `/QSPI_SS`. SW101.2 is GND. Pressing the button while the RP2040 drives chip select high creates contention and can disrupt execution from flash. R105, the existing 1 kΩ resistor, belongs to the crystal circuit. Raspberry Pi specifies a separate 1 kΩ resistor in the BOOTSEL branch close to the flash. This needs a schematic revision outside the original placement brief. [RP2040 hardware design guide, §2.2](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/hardware-design-with-rp2040.pdf)
- **Power is dominated by the switched LEDs.** J101 feeds the 0.5 A PTC F101. Fused `+5V` supplies the AP2112K linear regulator U101 and the TPS22917 load switch U105. Six LED drivers at a typical 12 mA per active color give about 216 mA of channel current when all channels are on. PWM edges and enable transients matter even at lower average brightness. The RP2040 and flash draw from the 3.3 V branch. There is no external switching-regulator inductor loop. [SK6812MINI-E-012 data sheet, pp. 3, 6](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf)
- **KiCad 10.0.8 found zero errors, zero unconnected items and zero schematic-parity differences on all three.** I ran DRC with all severities on project copies, first with saved fills and again after refilling. Warning counts were identical on both runs. No DRC exclusions were recorded. Each layout has the same U103 library-mismatch warning. Comparing the embedded QFN with the installed library showed matching pad positions, sizes, shapes and layers, and a difference in the length of two pin-one silk lines. All 42 movable parts are on B.Cu. The six locked switches and six locked LEDs have identical poses across candidates. Footprint counts, pad nets and compared footprint geometry agree. The original unrouted board and the requested designer NOTES.md files were absent from the supplied set, so the baseline check is relative to the other submissions.
- **Standard-process reference.** The files specify two 35 µm copper layers, a 1.51 mm FR-4 core and 1.6 mm total thickness. JLCPCB lists 0.10/0.10 mm track/space for two-layer 1 oz copper and 0.20 mm copper clearance to routed edges and slots. All three use 0.30 mm via drills. J101’s four plated shell slots are 0.60 mm wide and 1.40 or 1.70 mm long. The shared 0.4 mm-pitch QFN has 0.20 × 0.875 mm perimeter lands and 0.20 mm pad gaps. Confirm those lands in the fab/assembly DFM review given JLCPCB’s generic 0.25 × 0.25 mm minimum SMD-pad listing. [JLCPCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)

## Measurements

Coordinates are KiCad board coordinates in mm, viewed from the front, with y increasing downwards. Ground area is saved GND fill divided by board area inside Edge.Cuts after the six LED openings. Ground coverage under a route is the fraction of its centerline directly over opposite-layer GND fill. A gap can still have a lateral return through nearby copper, pads or stitching. These measurements identify detours and asymmetry but do not determine impedance or emissions.

The RP2040 uses USB full speed at 12 Mbit/s. On these short links I give more weight to the reference, spacing changes and stubs than to submillimetre skew. None establishes a controlled 90 Ω differential geometry over the whole path. Electrical compliance, assembled plug fit and oscillator startup remain unverified.

## B — second, 7/10 overall

| Category | Score | Findings |
|---|---:|---|
| EMI | 7 | The 94 GND vias include regularly spaced stitches in the key field, for example (151, 118), (155, 118) and (159, 118). SCLK has opposite GND under 98% of its route, and XIN/XOUT have direct fill along their MCU-side nets. The long BOOTSEL branch cuts F.Cu across the top, with 24.00 mm of `/QSPI_SS` at y = 101.00, 0.90 mm copper-to-edge. Along the bottom, fused `+5V` passes just 0.35 mm from the outline between (138.05, 137.55) and (141.55, 137.55). `/Key3/DOUT` follows the right edge for 19.05 mm, albeit over ground. |
| Signal integrity | 6 | Flash data and SCLK stay on B.Cu. SD1 is 5.30 mm, SD3 is 24.43 mm and SCLK is 21.28 mm. The dominant flash defect is the open-switch branch: U103.56 to U104.1 is only 3.64 mm, yet another 49.94 mm of copper runs from U104.1 at (115.61, 111.39) to SW101.1 at (142.15, 115.50), including 47.30 mm on F.Cu. USB A7 changes layer twice at (117.75, 132.35) and (119.30, 130.30), while D+ stays on B.Cu. The A7/B7 tie is 9.67 mm. D+/D− have a 1.70 mm edge gap at U102, falling to 0.20 mm on part of the MCU run. Y101 at (132.10, 119.00) has via-free, well-referenced legs, but the MCU-to-crystal traces are 8.93 mm on XIN and 12.15 mm through R105 on XOUT. |
| Power | 7 | B has the best regulator pin-to-cap lengths. U101 at (121.20, 131.45) reaches C102/C104 in 1.96/1.51 mm. U103.45 to C116.1 is 1.69 mm on B.Cu, with a GND via 0.80 mm from C116.2. The `+5V_LED` distribution uses 108.6 mm of 0.40 mm copper, 27.8 mm at 0.25 mm and two vias. Its main limitation is input distribution: F101.2 to U105.1 is 23.50 mm, and the 10 µF C101.1 at (154.70, 134.00) is 35.55 mm of trace from F101.2. U105 still has local input/output ceramics at 3.18/2.21 mm. |
| Placement | 7 | The 67.15 × 38.10 mm rounded rectangle is the smallest. J101 faces left on its x = 109.00 guide and U106 at (120.20, 110.65) is just 3.77 mm of copper from the MCU data output. Putting BOOTSEL at (139.00, 113.60) and the bulk capacitor between the bottom-row keys saves outline area at a real electrical cost. U103.10’s nearest routed 3.3 V capacitor is 6.51 mm away, despite other MCU pins having short, direct bypasses. |
| Routing and manufacturability | 6 | All 118 vias are 0.60/0.30 mm. Five drills sit in U103.57, including (120.00, 116.90). Others enter C106.2 at (127.00, 118.00), D501.1 at (131.00, 134.00) and C101.2 at (159.00, 134.00). Tenting cannot close the solder-side opening inside a land. **Before release:** relocate the BOOTSEL branch and input bulk capacitor, and move the drills out of solder lands. Those changes would preserve most of B’s area advantage and its good single-layer flash-clock route.
