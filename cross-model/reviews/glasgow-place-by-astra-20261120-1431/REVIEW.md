# Glasgow four-layer PCB release review

**Hold the fab order. Select A for release work.** A has complete connectivity, a largely uninterrupted signal reference, short regulator loops and a through-drilled layout. Two pairs of holes are below the fab's published spacing, copper approaches the routed edge too closely, and the USB geometry needs an agreed production stackup. C also connects the circuit, but needs an HDI build or a substantial reroute. B would short the FPGA core supply to ground. D lacks the routed power and clocks to run.

Overall scores reflect the consequences for a working product on the requested process. A supply short, hundreds of opens or a change of fabrication process weighs more than silkscreen polish. Placement was a design choice on all four boards.

## Checks common to the four projects

I opened each board with KiCad 10.0.8, inspected rendered top and bottom routing, measured pads, tracks, vias, outlines and saved fill polygons, and ran KiCad DRC on project copies with the accompanying rules. The saved-fill and fresh-refill runs agreed on violation counts by type and on missing connections. Coordinates below are KiCad absolute **(x, y) in mm**, with y increasing downward. F, L2, L3 and B denote F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu and B.Cu.

- **Mechanical and circuit baseline.** All four have the same 80 × 49 outline, spanning (50, 71) to (130, 120), the same 272 footprint UUIDs and library IDs, and matching normalized pad geometry, drills, layers and net assignments. J1–J10 and MK1–MK4 agree in location, orientation and side across all four. Examples are USB J1 (52.65, 95.50), IDC J2 (92.57, 77.72), IDC J3 (115.43, 113.28) and expansion J5 (72.50, 76.50). A leaves J6–J10 unlocked in the delivered file. There is no separate starting PCB in /work to certify every historical edit. No project includes the requested designer NOTES.md.
- **Schematic parity.** The three schematic sheets are identical across candidates. KiCad gives the same 421 parity warnings on each, including 199 pad-net name conflicts and two value-field warnings for C90/C91. I independently compared the exported schematic netlist with every board by reference and pin. All **1,063** named pin assignments form the same electrical groups, including schematic +3.3V versus board +3V3. The missing-connection counts above measure physical copper. All four projects also report 174 installed-library footprint mismatches and 77 footprint-library issues in this environment. The four shared component-type markers concern J1's shell anchors and the exposed-pad holes of U1, U8 and U36. C expressly excludes those four plus seven stacked drill locations.
- **Measurement conventions.** A whole-net length below sums track centrelines and includes branches. A named pad-to-pad path follows connected copper in the plane of the board, including pad-centre access where needed. Via counts are given separately from planar length. A projected distance between opposite-side pads measures placement, not loop inductance. Reference coverage is the length of a track centreline over actual adjacent-layer *filled zone copper*. Signal tracks, fringing fields and more distant layers can still carry return current in a gap. Length matching alone establishes neither impedance nor timing compliance.
- **USB and fabrication assumptions.** The USB target is nominally 90 Ω differential. For a straight 0.19 mm pair with a 0.15 mm edge gap, I estimated odd-mode impedance with a two-dimensional electrostatic cross-section using the file's 35 µm rectangular copper, FR-4 εr 4.5 and a continuous plane. The 0.48 mm outer dielectric in A/B gives about **116 Ω differential**. C's 0.135 mm gives about **92 Ω**. These are bare-microstrip estimates. Solder mask, neighbouring copper, launch geometry and the laminate actually ordered change the result. JLCPCB distinguishes regular mechanical drilling from HDI, publishes 0.20 mm via-hole spacing and routed-edge clearance, and flags some small via sizes for added cost. [JLCPCB manufacturing capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)
- **Power and interfaces.** U15 is the USB 0.5 A current-limit switch and data-line ESD protector. U8 supplies 3.3 V, U36 supplies 1.2 V, and U14/U31 are adjustable bank LDOs. The board has no buck-converter switch node. The important loops are the USB input, the LDO input/output capacitors and the transient current at the FX2, FPGA and bus translators. The schematic annotates 500 mA at 3.3 V, 300 mA at 1.2 V and up to 32 mA per translator rail. These are design ratings or load cases, not a claim they all occur simultaneously. U2/U3 are I²C EEPROMs. The faster parallel interface is the U1–U30 FIFO, and J5 carries 13 differential expansion pairs with external termination expected on the daughterboard.

## A — 8/10 overall

**EMI · 8/10.** L2 has a single 3,433 mm² GND fill with no routed tracks. Both top-side Z0 traces overlie it for their full runs. The top-side crystal output also has continuous GND beneath all 6.38 mm. At J1 the shell has its own /SHLD copper, with C13 at (58.85, 91.70) and R3 at (59.05, 99.15) to GND. On the reversible-contact fanout, 1.59 mm of top /USB_P overlies /SHLD and its 1.05 mm bottom link at x=55.50 has no L3 pour under it. The rest of the pair benefits from its short route to U1. The FIFO has a less satisfactory return. Bottom /D0 crosses L3 +3V3, +1V2 and a 2.23 mm gap around (77.56, 99.34). Local bypassing matters at those changes of reference. The 121 GND vias serve the circuitry, with none within 2 mm of the outline. The nearest USB/clock/crystal/J5-pair trace edge is 3.46 mm from the perimeter.

**Signal integrity · 8/10.** J1.A6/A7 to U1.8/9 measure 10.66/10.71 mm, both on F, with 0.19 mm width and 0.15 mm gap at x=60. The reverse B6/B7 paths are 14.12/11.81 mm with two vias each. TP15 (55.50, 96.30) and TP16 (57.90, 94.70) sit on the crossover vias, avoiding long probe branches. The pair spreads at U15. The nominal stackup is the principal USB impedance concern. Y1 (61.90, 92.40) and its two 9 pF load capacitors keep /XTALIN and /XTALOUT on F, 8.25/6.38 mm of total copper and no vias. From U30 to J5 all 26 Z legs fall between 28.52 and 29.60 mm. The Z0 legs are 29.60/28.95 mm, with the other twelve pairs matched within 0.05 mm in planar copper. /CLKREF is the exception to the otherwise disciplined fast routing, with 36.07 mm of branched copper on three layers and four vias, including (73.10, 90.45) near TP6. FIFO /D0–/D7 span 11.45–18.68 mm.

**Power · 9/10.** FB1 is beside U15, with only 1.29 mm of planar path from FB1.2 to U15.3. Of the +5V distribution, 114 mm is 0.75 mm wide, including the 25.60 mm L3 run from (116.30, 118.20) to (90.70, 118.20). U36.6 has C86's +5V pad 1.38 mm away on F, and U36.1 reaches C27.1 by 1.90 mm of F routing. U8 has a similarly compact input/output arrangement. The back-side 100 nF capacitors align with individual FX2 supply pins. C3.1 is only 0.05 mm in projection from U1.7 at (65.85, 95.25), with a supply via at (64.90, 95.25) and the nearby plated return array in U1's exposed pad. At the bank-A regulator, local copper spreads the output and shunt current around R56 (122.55, 90.40) and R49 (122.55, 87.60), while the feedback net totals just 3.41 mm on F. This keeps load-current copper separate from much of the sense routing.

**Placement · 8/10.** The physical order J1 → U15 → U1 (69.85, 95.50) → U30 (83.70, 95.50) keeps the USB and FIFO connections short. Repeated translator cells have capacitors on the reverse side of their supply pins, and the status LEDs align with the front legend. D20 (104.95, 108.65) is close to exposed bank signals but also to the J3 shroud. Its drawn fab body ends at y=109.375 and J3's begins at y=110.05, about 0.68 mm apart despite overlapping courtyards. Other overlaps include C23/C77 beneath U30 and U14/D18 at the bank supply. The 68 courtyard markers merit assembly and rework review, especially if the optional DNP sockets are fitted.

**Routing and manufacture · 7/10.** All 416 vias are through-drilled. The smallest land/drill is 0.40/0.20 mm and the minimum trace is 0.15 mm. KiCad finds zero opens and shorts, one dangling R40-net via at (70.61, 113.31), and 16 hole-spacing warnings. Two VIOB pairs leave only **0.15 mm drill-edge spacing**, one at (97.05, 106.95)/(97.50, 106.95), the other at (107.30, 105.40)/(107.75, 105.40). /SHLD reaches x=50.0505 at the x=50 edge. I tried a 0.25 mm edge rule and refilled a disposable copy. It retained zero opens and exposed SW1 pad 3 at (74.65, 119.23), just **0.17 mm** from the edge. The routing is generally orderly, with a small 45° included-angle GND jog at (72.015, 93.335) on B. The “B2” silk at (94.50, 99.70) clips U23 pads. Before release, separate the two tight drill pairs, resolve the edge copper and switch clearance, check the tight assembly envelopes, and agree the USB stackup before generating production artwork.

## C — 6/10 overall

**EMI · 5/10.** C provides outer GND fills, 201 GND vias, 24 of them within 2 mm of the edge, and dedicated L3 ground under much of the USB and expansion areas. For example, 27.2 mm of the 28.5 mm bottom Z9_N run overlies GND. The outer pours have many separate pieces and L2 carries **437.6 mm of routed tracks** through its nominal ground reference, leaving 13 GND fill polygons. /CLKIF alone occupies 23.9 mm on L2 between escapes near (64.85, 98.25) and (82.20, 94.30). All 8.24 mm of bottom /D0 misses L3 pour, including the area around (77.38, 97.88). This puts simultaneous FIFO edges over a much less predictable return than the edge stitching suggests. The nearest fast trace is 3.55 mm from the perimeter, and the fills respect about 0.25 mm edge setback.

**Signal integrity · 7/10.** The top USB A6/A7 paths to U1 are both 9.51 mm. At x=63 the centrelines are y=95.75 and 96.09, giving the intended 0.15 mm gap. The proposed stack is credible for the straight section. The reverse paths measure 13.03/13.57 mm in plane, but B6 stays on F while B7 traverses the board twice via (55.47, 94.75) and (57.96, 96.01). The vertical delay and the taps to back-side U15 and TP15/16 make that orientation less symmetric. All 13 J5 pairs match to within 0.001 mm in summed planar track length. About 64% of Z5_P is within 0.50 mm centre-to-centre of Z5_N on the same layer, versus 85% on A, sampling the positive leg at intervals of at most 0.1 mm. /D0 uses seven vias and all four layers. /XTALOUT takes two vias and 8.54 mm on B out of 11.60 mm total, crossing both +3V3 and GND reference areas between U1 and the Y1/C11 circuit near (63.30, 92.05). The matched USB and LVDS sections earn the score, but several other fast nets pay for that routing space.

**Power · 5/10.** The four FPGA core balls have +1V2 capacitor pads within 0.44–0.49 mm in projection, a strong placement choice. At the other end of the rail, U36.1 (67.75, 111.65) reaches its closest output capacitor C27.1 (72.30, 105.59) over 8.04 mm of F copper. U31.1 (126.10, 89.95) is 7.60 mm from even the closest +5V capacitor pad. U15 sits on B with FB1 and C8 on F. The /xVBUS network uses 0.15 mm traces and three layers. +5V has no plane, and the U15.4–U31.1 path measures about 124 mm through seven vias, predominantly 0.25 mm wide. Even at a full 0.25 mm width, that length of 35 µm copper is about 0.24 Ω at room temperature before via resistance. Shared load current makes that consequential for bank voltage headroom. The bank-A /ISNS_H network adds 14.53 mm of 0.15 mm copper near R49 (126.20, 82.30), without A's local shunt pours.

**Placement · 8/10.** U1 (70.00, 95.50) and U30 (84.95, 95.50) keep the FIFO local, D22 is on B at (98.90, 82.20) clear of the top IDC shroud, and all 11 courtyard warnings involve fixed mechanical features or headers. C18.1 sits directly behind U30.D4 with a 0.44 mm offset. U1.55 at (67.25, 91.50) is 2.69 mm from the nearest +3V3 capacitor pad, and C27 is remote from U36. There are 154 footprint instances on B, including the USB protection and bank circuitry, so both sides matter to assembly and inspection.

**Routing and manufacture · 4/10.** Zero opens, shorts or active board DRC errors, and just four mask-clipped silk warnings. The minimum trace is 0.127 mm. The drill stack requires **666 through vias, 113 laser microvias and 44 buried L2–L3 vias**. Seven drill locations are intentionally stacked, including /SCL at (84.50, 97.50). There are also 0.20 mm through drills in FPGA solder lands, e.g. U30.F8 +3V3 at (84.90, 97.05). Planarized filled/capped structures and via-in-pad assembly need explicit fabrication instructions and an HDI quote. Suppressing co-located-hole markers cannot specify that process to the fab. There are tight reversals, including a 45° included angle on Z4_N at (86.95, 93.50) on F, but the process change is the much larger cost and yield issue. **Do not submit C as an ordinary four-layer through-via order.**

## B — 3/10 overall

**EMI · 7/10.** An unrouted L2 provides a single 3,456 mm² GND polygon. /IO_Banks/Z0_P and /IO_Banks/Z0_N run entirely above it on F. U15 (61.00, 98.10), C8 (61.20, 100.50) and FB1 (58.60, 99.60) stay together, although /xVBUS takes 10.0 mm on B. L3 is split between +3V3, +5V, VIOA, VIOB and GND and also carries 284.7 mm of tracks. On B, Z5_N crosses both GND and +3V3 and has a 3.67 mm unpoured stretch near (83.29, 86.37). The bottom /USB_N fanout mostly references +3V3 instead of the L2 ground seen by the top pair. Of 135 GND vias only one is within 2 mm of the edge. The nearest fast trace is comfortably set back at 4.31 mm.

**Signal integrity · 7/10.** J1.A6/A7 to U1.8/9 each measure about 9.58 mm on F. The 0.19 mm traces have a 0.15 mm gap around (61.00, 95.50). The same stackup concern applies as on A. On the reverse contacts and probe branches, /USB_P and /USB_N have 4.36/4.81 mm on B, and their vias at (58.75, 95.08) and (61.00, 96.10) are staggered. On P the first of those lands reaches the main trace centred at y=95.33 through the rim of the via. The 24 MHz crystal is a better feature, with /XTALIN and /XTALOUT just 6.90/6.72 mm of top copper and no vias at Y1 (61.90, 92.20). /CLKIF takes two vias and 14.5 mm on L3. FIFO data span 13.12–23.09 mm. J5 Z0 measures 16.87/17.83 mm and its different pairs range from about 17 to 35 mm. KiCad also reports U30's /VCCPLL1 and /GNDPLL1 connections open, so the good pair geometry gives no assurance of a working clock system.

**Power · 2/10.** The +5V backbone includes a sensible 0.80 mm B trace from (118.55, 117.75) to (118.55, 87.21). U8.6 is 1.36 mm from C26.2 and U36.1 is 2.33 mm of copper from C27.1. But a **+1V2 track shorts to C14.2 GND** at (77.8125, 104.40), and the core distribution includes 26.9 mm of 0.127 mm copper, including the 6.5 mm L3 segment at x=84.90. U30.C6 at (87.30, 95.50) and C79.1 at (88.50, 95.985) are physically close but electrically disconnected on /VCCPLL1. The additional +1V2 open and PLL return opens must be fixed before power-up.

**Placement · 8/10.** The FX2's nine +3V3 supply pins are each within 0.73–0.82 mm in projection of a capacitor pad. U4 (96.45, 85.70) has its two rail bypasses immediately behind or beside it. Y1 and the bank regulators have compact supporting parts. U31 (122.30, 89.00), its shunt R49 (126.70, 88.90) and monitor U21 (121.90, 84.35) stay in the same bank. The top-side LEDs line up with the status legend and SW1 is at the accessible south edge. There are 24 courtyard conflicts, and TP3 (91.50, 108.50) clips the bank-B label. C14 and R45 also show why last-mile placement changes must be checked against copper on the back.

**Routing and manufacture · 2/10.** The 476 vias are all through-drilled, the smallest land/drill is 0.40/0.20 mm and most bends are ordinary mitres. There is a 45° included turn in +1V2 on B at (86.50, 93.90). The 15 opens span 11 nets, including /SCL, /~{CY_RESET} and three bank-A direction nets. Besides C14, a +3V3 via at (89.61, 106.90) shorts R45.1, Net-(R45-Pad1), and leaves only 0.0812 mm drill-to-pad clearance. There are two short markers, two copper-clearance errors, two mask-bridge errors, one hole-clearance error and 13 dangling tracks. The L3 +3V3 fill reaches x=50.0505 near (50.05, 91.22). Neither ordinary via technology nor a wide +5V trunk outweighs the opens and shorts.

## D — 1/10 overall

**EMI · 3/10.** L2 is a single 3,518 mm² GND fill and its only routed tracks are GND. L3 has a single 3,258 mm² +3V3 fill. Those are promising reference surfaces, but their apparent continuity reflects how much circuitry is missing. Despite 210 GND vias, U15.2 GND (61.10, 91.50), a Y1 case pad and U30.F5 GND all appear in missing connections. The long bottom USB branches reference +3V3 and return to F at different places. Z3_N runs on F at y=71.70 near x=79, giving only 0.625 mm from copper edge to board edge. The oscillator, regulator and FPGA switching-current loops cannot close as drawn.

**Signal integrity · 2/10.** The direct A-contact runs are only about 5.7 mm, but at x=59 the 0.30 mm USB tracks have a **1.20 mm edge gap**. Branches from the through path to TP15 (60.35, 88.05) and TP16 (60.35, 86.00) are about 8.47 and 12.74 mm respectively, each with two layer changes. Total P/N copper is 16.62/21.76 mm and two/four vias. The class specifies a 0.45 mm pair width while the board actually uses 0.20 and 0.30 mm. /XTALIN, /XTALOUT, /CLKIF, /CLKREF and six of /D0–/D7 have **no tracks**. Even where there is copper, Z9_P/N measures 33.41/65.93 mm and six/two vias. This leaves no functioning FX2–FPGA timing path to qualify.

**Power · 1/10.** +5V, VIOA and VIOB have neither tracks nor zones. DRC reports 18 +5V, 30 VIOA and 31 VIOB missing connections. FB1 and U15 have some 0.25 mm input copper, but U15.4 cannot feed the LDOs. The entire +1V2 network consists of four 0.15 mm FPGA escape tracks totalling 2.26 mm and four vias, with no core plane. U36.1 at (70.95, 107.35) lies just 0.25 mm in projection from back-side C14.1, while U30.D8 (84.60, 99.60) is 15.40 mm from the nearest core capacitor pad. Neither geometry delivers power without the missing rail connections.

**Placement · 3/10.** U1 is reasonably close to J1, and some translator bypass pads sit directly behind their pins. Other capacitor groups occupy spare board area far from their loads, including C18/C24 near y=84.20 for an FPGA centred at (83.00, 98.00). Y1 at (64.60, 87.50) leaves a 6.40 mm straight-line gap between C12.1 and U1.5 even before routing. D4 ACT and D5 ERR are on the **back** at (71.10, 72.20) and (74.30, 72.20), while their front legends remain at (53.00, 87.00) and (53.00, 89.00). The 150 µF can C87 at (60.75, 107.10) is also on B, so its 5.9 mm package height requires underside enclosure clearance. The favorable count of 11 courtyard conflicts misses both usability problems.

**Routing and manufacture · 1/10.** There are 434 through vias, minimum land/drill 0.45/0.20 mm, and 0.15 mm minimum tracks. A 45° included turn on Z9_N at (81.40, 92.825) is minor beside **321 missing connections across 138 nets**, 33 dangling tracks and five dangling vias. For example U30.H9 /SCL at (81.40, 100.40) is isolated from routed /SCL copper. A 0.25 mm drill at (61.15, 99.02) sits in C3's GND solder pad. The L3 +3V3 fill reaches x=50.0505, and silk has 199 mask clips, including the “A6” label over U11.3 near (94.75, 93.95). A standard fab could reproduce much of this copper, but populating it would yield an inoperative board.

## Ranking and authorship

1. **A** has the shortest path to a working standard-process order. The remaining risks are identifiable drill, edge, impedance and assembly details.
2. **C** connects the whole circuit and handles the principal USB and LVDS runs deliberately. The HDI dependency, interrupted FIFO reference and resistive supply routing put it behind A.
3. **B** has a good floorplan, crystal and main USB route. The core short, second short and missing functional nets outweigh those strengths.
4. **D** needs substantial power and signal routing before bring-up or a meaningful system-level SI test.

**I think A is the human layout (97% confidence).** My strongest evidence is how the routing priorities change with the circuit. A spends area on broad shunt and LDO copper, uses the backs of the FX2 and translators for individual bypasses, puts the USB probe pads on crossover vias, and tunes the expansion pairs across lanes as well as within each pair. It achieves all of this with mechanical through holes. The tight but physically separated D20/J3 envelopes also look like a deliberate assembly tradeoff. This is an inference from the finished layout, made after scoring.
