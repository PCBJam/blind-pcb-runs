# USB width and stackup trial

A two-dimensional electrostatic model of an outer pair over the supplied 0.48 mm dielectric gave about 111 Ω for the original 0.19 mm traces and 0.15 mm gap. I tried 0.20 mm outer dielectrics, keeping the overall board at 1.60 mm. The revised USB trunk uses 0.245 mm traces with a 0.155 mm gap. Its first approach to U1 came within 0.1275 mm of a 3.3 V via. I discarded that endpoint geometry. The earlier atlc cross-section sweep also produced inconsistent results, so I checked the estimate with an independent finite-difference model at two mesh sizes.
