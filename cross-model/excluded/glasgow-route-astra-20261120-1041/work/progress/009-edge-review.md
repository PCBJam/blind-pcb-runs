# Routed-edge clearance

A 0.30 mm copper setback improved the original 0.05 mm rule and the pour setback. The trial exposed three exceptions at the reset switch. I moved the two movable shell vias inward. SW1 pad 3 belongs to the fixed footprint and has 0.17 mm of copper-to-outline clearance. A rule specific to that pad records the required fabrication capability. I also raised narrow zones to a 0.15 mm minimum fill thickness and verified connectivity after refill.
