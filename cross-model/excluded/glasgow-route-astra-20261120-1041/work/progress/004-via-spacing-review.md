# Power via drill spacing

The four +5 V barrels above U14 illustrate the first DRC result. A 0.30 mm drill at 0.50 mm pitch leaves a 0.20 mm web. The project requires 0.25 mm. I chose to spread the affected via arrays and retain their original drill and copper diameters. The other flagged sites were on 3.3 V, GND and the adjustable I/O supplies.
