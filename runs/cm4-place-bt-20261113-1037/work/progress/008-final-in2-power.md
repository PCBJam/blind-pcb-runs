# 008 – In2: ground with power islands
In2 is a ground plane with islands: 12 V trunk + neck, one 3.3 V island per M.2 socket, 1.2 V under the switch, small 3.3 VA and RP2040-core islands, and the 5 V shape (5 V buck -> CM4 pins, strip to the fan headers and the LDO). It stays ground under every bottom-side PCIe hop.
