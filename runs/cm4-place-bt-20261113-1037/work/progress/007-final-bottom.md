# 007 – final board, bottom copper
Bottom carries the M.2 bucks under the sockets, the 12 V trunk pour along the top edge (stitched to the same shape on In2), the switch bucks and decoupling, and the long slow signals between CM4, RP2040, LEDs and sockets. The rest is ground pour, stitched to In1 on a 6 mm grid (184 vias) plus a via at almost every ground pad.
