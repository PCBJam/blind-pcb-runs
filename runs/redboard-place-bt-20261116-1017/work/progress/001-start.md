# 001 – starting point
Board as received: outline, connectors, buttons, standoffs, fiducials and silk labels locked; ~200 parts parked below the outline; no copper.
Read the netlist and the three schematic sheets, rendered top/bottom silk (3D render) to see what the locked labels imply:
bottom labels D1±/D2±/VUSB1/VUSB2/BOOT → test points on the bottom next to them; SHLD1/2, I2C, CHG, PWR, LED_DO → the solder jumpers on the bottom;
top labels CHG/PWR/RGB-IO12 → the three LEDs; the "ESP32-C5" bracket and the free right edge between the standoffs → the C5 module with its antenna at the right board edge.
