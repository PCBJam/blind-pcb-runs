# H4E2 routing notes

Result: `/work/H4E2.kicad_pcb` is fully routed (2 layers, 0 unconnected). KiCad 10.0.8 DRC with the
project rules + `H4E2.kicad_dru` reports no clearance, short, edge, hole, annular or unconnected
violations. The only messages left are the ones the unrouted board already had: the missing
`HALE-git` footprint library (10 warnings), the two logo footprints (duplicate/extra footprint) and
the excluded NPTH-in-courtyard under the display. The final report is `routing/drc_final.rpt`.

No footprint was moved, rotated, flipped, added or removed (checked by script against the original).

## Approach

Nearly every part sits on the back, so:

- **B.Cu** carries the components, almost all signal routing and all power distribution.
- **F.Cu** is a ground plane, broken only where the placement makes a crossing unavoidable.
- GND is poured on both layers. Every SMD ground pad has its own short stub and via into the F.Cu
  plane (54 of them), plus about 150 stitching vias on a ~6 mm staggered grid.

The routing was generated by scripts in `/work/routing` (a small 0.1 mm grid A* router that I steered
net by net with waypoints, plus hand-placed polylines for the display bus). `routing/run.sh`
regenerates the board from `routing/unrouted.kicad_pcb`.

## Widths, vias, rules

| Net group | Width | Via |
|---|---|---|
| +5V, +5V_LED | 0.6 mm (0.3–0.35 mm on two low-current spurs, see below) | 0.8 / 0.4 |
| +5V_AUX, LDO output | 0.5 mm | – |
| +3.3VA | 0.4 mm | – |
| +3.3V | 0.3 mm (only the MCP23017 and pull-ups; has to fit a 0.65 mm pitch pin) | – |
| Hall outputs, ADC inputs | 0.25 mm | none |
| Other signals | 0.2 mm | 0.6 / 0.3 |
| GND stubs | 0.4 mm (0.25 mm on fine-pitch pins) | 0.6 / 0.3 |

- Clearance is 0.2 mm everywhere; tracks stay 0.4 mm from the board edge and 0.3 mm from the LED cut-outs.
- Zones: 0.3 mm clearance, 0.25 mm minimum width, thermal reliefs on pads (the footprints are
  hand-solder variants), solid on vias, isolated islands removed.
- Project file changes:
  - Net classes `Power` and `Analog` added with patterns.
  - Board minimums tightened to what was actually used, which is also comfortably inside JLCPCB's
    standard 2-layer capability: clearance 0.15, track 0.15, copper-to-edge 0.3, hole clearance 0.25,
    via annular 0.13, via diameter 0.5.
  - `H4E2.kicad_dru` is untouched.

## Decisions and why

- **Display bus on F.Cu.** The XIAO pin-out interleaves the display pins with the ADC SPI pins on
  the left edge, and BL/CS/RES are on the far side of the module, so the two buses cannot both stay
  on B.Cu. The ADC SPI stays on B.Cu. The six display signals go to F.Cu with one via each, run as a
  tight 0.5 mm-pitch bus along the north band and drop into the through-hole header from the east.
  That keeps the F.Cu plane solid under the ADC and its analog parts. RES squeezes between header
  pins 6 and 7 and enters pin 5 from the west so no second via is needed.
- **ADC SPI** enters the MCP3208 from underneath its body, in pin order, with no vias.
- **Analog.** The four hall outputs run on B.Cu with no vias, over unbroken F.Cu ground, in a channel
  down the west edge. The key cells are entered so that the order matches the ADC resistor order.
  +3.3VA goes from the ferrite past the ADC, down the west edge and around the south and east of the
  key block as a comb, also with no vias.
- **LED power and data hop over the analog nets, not the other way round.** There are 8 short
  F.Cu jumpers (via pairs) in the key and encoder-LED area: 2 on +5V_LED, 6 on LED data.
- **+5V to the LED switch** crosses the module on F.Cu along the north edge (0.6 mm, 0.8 mm vias).
  EN_AUX and EN_LED also cross on F.Cu.
- **I2C.** SDA makes one 4 mm F.Cu hop next to the module so SDA and SCL arrive at the MCP23017 in
  pin order and enter from under its body. C3001 blocks the outside approach.
- **Encoder 2 signals** (A6/B6/SW6) run on F.Cu from the MCP23017 to the encoder, two vias each.
  They are slow and RC-filtered, and the B.Cu pour underneath is solid.
- **Encoder 1 A/B** make a short F.Cu hop (two vias each) over the E1_SW track, which runs on B.Cu
  down the west side of the encoder.
- **Level-shifter supply** passes between the pads of R1001 at 0.35 mm; it was the only planar way in.
- **+5V test point** TP1001 is fed by a 0.3 mm track between the pad rows of U1001.
- **No vias under the XIAO module.** One B.Cu track (+3.3V) runs under it, because pad 12 cannot
  escape outward past R1005.

## Known weaknesses / not done

- **Router artefacts.** Pad centres are off the 0.1 mm grid, so many pads have a 0.05 mm stub inside
  the pad and there are about 200 segments shorter than 0.15 mm, plus a few small jogs (for example
  E_INT/E1_SW beside R3004). They are DRC-clean and electrically harmless, but a hand-finished board
  would be tidier. No teardrops, no arcs.
- **F.Cu plane cuts.** The display bus cuts a slot about 50 mm long (ground wraps around both ends
  and the B.Cu pour is stitched on both sides). Under the XIAO the bus tracks sit at pad pitch, and
  the strips between them are unfilled because I allow no vias there. The three encoder-2 tracks cut
  a long diagonal across the lower right.
- **Display SPI is unverified.** It runs about 45–55 mm on F.Cu next to BL/CS/RES with 0.3 mm gaps
  and no length matching. That should be fine for an SPI TFT, but I did no signal-integrity analysis.
  If the panel is clocked very fast, a series resistor at the MCU would be the first thing to add
  (not possible with fixed placement).
- **+3.3VA is long**, roughly 140 mm to the farthest hall sensor at 0.4 mm. The drop is in the
  millivolt range at sensor currents and each sensor has its local 100 nF, but it is a loop around
  the key block, not a star.
- **+5V_LED reaches the key LEDs the long way** (down the east edge, then west), roughly 110 mm at
  0.6 mm, with two via-pair hops. Fine for six SK6812s, not generous.
- **B.Cu ground is fragmented** between tracks in the key area; pieces without a via were dropped by
  island removal. F.Cu is the real ground plane.
- The stitching grid is mechanical, not placed by judgement.
- Silkscreen was not reviewed or adjusted. Vias are tented (board default).
- Gerber/drill export for ordering was not produced; only a test plot was run to confirm the board
  plots.
